# Accessibility notes

The theme starts in a readable Writer profile. Source Notes adds a reference-like surface. Large Type raises the base text size.

- Main text begins at 18px; Large Type begins at 22px.
- Controls work with keyboard, touch, and visible focus.
- Reading settings offer text scaling, high contrast, and reduced motion.
- Activities have no timer, score pressure, flashing, account, or data collection.
- These activities are optional practice. They do not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.

Test with a screen reader and the target reader before public use. Add alt text to every WordPress image.
