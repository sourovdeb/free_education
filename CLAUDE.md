# Free Education — EU Open Education Opportunities

## Project Summary
Discover and surface "hidden" open education opportunities for Europeans within the EU. Most resources exist but aren't widely visible—lesser-known MOOCs, microcredentials, funded mobility, and nationally funded open courses.

## Goals
- Map public EU education portals and identify visibility gaps
- Compile a searchable index or newsletter of hidden opportunities
- Provide user-friendly guides and outreach for target audience
- Help poor and talented Europeans access education without gatekeeping

## Audience
Europeans seeking free/low-cost open education: scholarships, MOOCs, microcredentials, mobility programs (Erasmus+, national schemes).

## Key Initiatives

### 1. Source Mapping & Research
- Erasmus+ portal, European Education Area resources
