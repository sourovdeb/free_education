# 🔬 Psychology & Mental Health Claims Audit

**What it does:** Researches and audits a claim about human psychology, neuroscience, marketing influence, or mental health treatment. Each entry: locates primary research, checks methodology/sample size, investigates funding and conflicts of interest, compares against independent/opposing research, and publishes a short, sourced verdict.

**Frequency:** As needed
**Output format:** Markdown (.md) — one file per audited claim
**Status:** ✅ Active

---

## Process

1. Identify a specific, checkable claim (not a vague trend).
2. Locate primary sources — peer-reviewed papers, not secondary write-ups — and note sample size and design.
3. Check funding: pharma, insurance, university, government, or advocacy-group sponsorship.
4. Flag patterns where conclusions align suspiciously well with a funder's (or author's) interests — financial *or* ideological.
5. Compare against at least one independent or opposing study.
6. Publish a 200–300 word micro-blog summary with citations, funding disclosure, and a plain verdict on whether the claim holds up.

If sources can't be verified or bias can't be assessed, that uncertainty is stated explicitly rather than glossed over.
