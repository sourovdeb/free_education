# Routine 3: Psychology / Neuroscience / Mental-Health Claim Audits

**What it does:** Researches and audits public claims about human psychology, neuroscience, marketing influence, and mental health treatment. For each claim, it locates primary research, checks methodology and sample size, investigates funding (pharma, insurers, universities, government), flags commercial/political bias patterns, compares against independent or opposing research, and publishes a short (200–300 word) sourced audit as a news item.

**Process per item:**
1. Identify the core claim/topic.
2. Locate primary sources; verify methodology and sample size.
3. Investigate funding and conflicts of interest.
4. Assess for bias favoring the funder's interests.
5. Compare against independent/opposing research.
6. Draft a cited, funding-disclosed micro-blog post.
7. Publish the post as a news item.

**Frequency:** As scheduled
**Output format:** Markdown (.md), one file per audited claim
**Status:** ✅ Active

## Contents

| File | Claim audited |

## Notes

- Every audit cites at least two independent sources and discloses each source's funding where available.
- If funding or independence cannot be verified, or evidence is clearly one-sided due to a funder conflict, the audit states that explicitly rather than presenting the claim as settled.
- This routine does not have automated WordPress publishing wired up (unlike Routine 1); posts are committed here as the canonical draft, ready to be copied into a CMS or reviewed before external publication.
