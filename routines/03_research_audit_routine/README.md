# Routine 3 — Research Audit

**What it does:** Audits a claim in psychology, neuroscience, marketing influence, or mental-health treatment: locates the primary research, checks methodology and sample size, investigates funding/conflicts of interest on all sides, compares against opposing or neutral research, and publishes a short, sourced micro-blog verdict.

**Process per audit:**
1. Identify the core claim.
2. Locate primary sources (peer-reviewed papers, not secondary reporting) and check methodology/sample size.
3. Investigate funding — pharma, insurers, universities, government — and note conflicts of interest.
4. Check for bias patterns that favor a funder's interests.
5. Compare against independent or opposing research.
6. Publish a 200–300 word micro-blog with citations, funding disclosure, and a robustness verdict. Anything that can't be independently verified is flagged as unverified rather than stated as fact.
