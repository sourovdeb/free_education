# 🧠 Psychology & Mental Health Claim Audits

**What it does:** Researches a psychology, neuroscience, marketing-influence, or mental-health-treatment claim end to end: locates primary sources, checks methodology/sample size, investigates funder conflicts of interest, compares against opposing/independent research, and publishes a short, sourced, funding-disclosed micro-blog verdict.

**Process per audit:**
1. Identify the core claim
2. Locate primary sources; verify methodology and sample size
3. Investigate funding (pharma, insurers, universities, government) and conflicts of interest
4. Assess for bias patterns favoring funders
5. Compare against neutral/opposing research
6. Draft a 200–300 word micro-blog with citations and funding disclosure
7. Publish as a news item (pending explicit approval to post externally — see note below)

**Frequency:** As needed
**Output format:** Markdown (audit + micro-blog draft per claim)
**Status:** ✅ Active

## Note on external publishing

Audits in this folder are drafted and committed here first. Actually publishing a post live (e.g., to sourovdeb.com via the WordPress publisher pattern used in `01_elt365_lessons_routine`) is a separate, explicit step — these posts touch medical/mental-health claims, so they are held for human review before anything goes out under the site's name, even as a draft post.
