# Writing Effective Email Subject Lines | Business English B1

**Level:** B1 | **Audience:** Professionals | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Email subject lines

---

## Your subject line is why your email gets opened — or ignored.

### Make it specific
- Weak: "Update" → Strong: "Q3 Sales Report — Ready for Review by Friday"
- Weak: "Question" → Strong: "Clarification needed: Budget approval deadline"

### Keep it under 50 characters
Many mobile screens cut off anything longer. Count before you send.

### Use action words when you need a response
- "Action required: sign off by [date]"
- "Decision needed: project timeline"
- "Please review: draft attached"

### Avoid
- ALL CAPS — reads as shouting
- Vague words: "update", "info", "hi"
- Starting with "RE:" when it is not a reply

## Practice

Rewrite these subject lines to make them specific and action-oriented:
1. "About the meeting"
2. "Question about the budget"
3. "FYI"
