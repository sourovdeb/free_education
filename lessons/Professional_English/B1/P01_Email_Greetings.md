# Professional Email Greetings and Sign-offs | Business English B1

**Level:** B1 | **Audience:** Professionals | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Email writing

---

## The right greeting makes a first impression.

### Formal (you don't know the person well)
- "Dear Mr Smith," / "Dear Ms Johnson,"
- "Dear Sir or Madam," (when you don't know the name)

### Semi-formal (you know them professionally)
- "Dear James," / "Hi Sarah,"

### Sign-offs
| Register | Sign-off |
|---|---|
| Formal (know name) | Yours sincerely, |
| Formal (don't know name) | Yours faithfully, |
| Semi-formal | Kind regards, / Best regards, |
| Professional but relaxed | Best, / Thanks, |

## The rule

Match the greeting to your relationship. "Hey Mike" to your director is too casual. "Dear Sir or Madam" to a colleague of three years is too stiff.

## Practice

Write the opening and closing of two emails: (a) to a new client you have never met; (b) to a colleague you speak to daily.
