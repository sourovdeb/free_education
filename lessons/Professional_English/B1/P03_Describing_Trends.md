# Describing Trends and Data | Business English B1

**Level:** B1 | **Audience:** Professionals | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Data and trends language

---

## Talking about data is a core professional skill.

### Verbs for movement
- ↑ **rise, increase, grow, climb, improve**
- ↓ **fall, drop, decrease, decline, reduce**
- → **remain stable, plateau, level off, stay constant**

### Adverbs for speed or degree
sharply · steadily · gradually · slightly · significantly · dramatically

## Example sentences

- "Sales **rose sharply** in Q3."
- "The number of users **gradually declined** over six months."
- "Profits **remained stable** throughout the year."

## Structure for describing a graph

1. What it shows (overview)
2. The main trend
3. A notable detail or exception

## Practice

Describe this trend in two sentences: Website traffic went from 10,000 visitors in January to 85,000 in June, with a small dip in March.
