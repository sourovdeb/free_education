# Making and Responding to Requests | Business English B1

**Level:** B1 | **Audience:** Professionals | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Workplace requests

---

## How you ask matters as much as what you ask.

### Making requests (most formal → least formal)
- "Would it be possible to send the report by Friday?"
- "Could you please forward the details?"
- "I was wondering if you could help with this."
- "Can you send me the file?" (fine with close colleagues)

### Accepting requests
- "Of course, I will get that to you by end of day."
- "Certainly. No problem."
- "Happy to help."

### Politely declining or delaying
- "I am afraid I am tied up this week, but I could manage next Wednesday."
- "I would love to help, but my schedule is full until [date]. Could someone else cover this?"

## Practice

Write a polite request to your manager for two extra days to complete a project. Start with: "Would it be possible to..."
