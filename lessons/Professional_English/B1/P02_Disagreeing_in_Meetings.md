# Politely Disagreeing in Meetings | Business English B1

**Level:** B1 | **Audience:** Professionals | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Meetings communication

---

## In English, disagreeing often requires softening language.

Direct disagreement can sound rude even when it is not intended. These phrases let you push back professionally.

## Useful phrases

- "That is an interesting point. However, I think..."
- "I see where you are coming from, but I would suggest..."
- "I am not sure I agree entirely. Could we also consider...?"
- "With respect, I think there may be another angle here."

## What to avoid

- "That is wrong." — too blunt
- "No." — alone, with nothing added
- Silence — often misread as agreement

## Key technique

Name their point first, then disagree. This shows you heard them and takes the sharpness out of your objection.

## Practice

Your colleague says: "We should cut the marketing budget by 50%." Disagree politely using one phrase from above. Write your response.
