# Telephone English — Leaving and Taking Messages | Business English B1

**Level:** B1 | **Audience:** Professionals | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Telephone communication

---

## Phone calls require specific phrases that emails do not.

### Calling and asking for someone
- "Could I speak to [name], please?"
- "I am calling regarding..."
- "Is [name] available?"

### When the person is not there
- "I am afraid [name] is in a meeting. Can I take a message?"
- "Would you like to leave a message?"

### Leaving a message
- "Could you tell them [your name] called? My number is [...]."
- "Could you ask them to call me back?"

### Taking a message
- "I will make sure they get the message."
- "I will pass that on."

## Clear tip

Always confirm the caller's name and number by repeating them back: "So that is James Hill, on 07..."

## Practice

Role-play: A calls for B, who is not available. A leaves a message. B returns the call. Use the phrases above throughout.
