# Presenting Findings Clearly | Business English B2

**Level:** B2 | **Audience:** Professionals | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Presentation language

---

## A clear structure lets your data speak.

### Signposting language
- **Opening:** "I would like to walk you through our main findings today."
- **Moving on:** "Turning now to..." · "This brings me to..."
- **Emphasising:** "The key point here is..." · "What this shows is..."
- **Summarising:** "In summary..." · "To wrap up..."
- **Questions:** "I am happy to take questions now."

### The three-part structure
1. **Tell them what you will tell them** (overview)
2. **Tell them** (the data or findings)
3. **Tell them what you told them** (summary and implications)

### Common mistakes
- Reading directly from slides — the slides support you, not replace you
- Too much detail, too little signposting
- No clear "So what?" at the end

## Practice

Prepare a 2-minute verbal presentation of any data you know. Use at least 4 signposting phrases. Record yourself and listen back.
