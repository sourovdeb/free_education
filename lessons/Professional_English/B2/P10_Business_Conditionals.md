# Conditional Sentences in a Business Context | Business English B2

**Level:** B2 | **Audience:** Professionals | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Business conditionals

---

## Conditionals are everywhere in professional English.

Proposals, negotiations, forecasts, and risk assessments all use them.

### Second conditional — hypothetical present or future
- "If we **reduced** the price by 10%, we **would** see higher volume."
- "If our competitor **entered** the market, we **would need** to review strategy."

### Third conditional — hypothetical past
- "If we **had launched** in Q1, we **would have captured** a larger share."

### Mixed conditional — past cause, present result
- "If we **had invested** earlier, we **would be** in a stronger position now."

### Business use cases
- **Risk:** "If the supplier delivers late, production would stall."
- **Proposal:** "If you approved an extra budget of X, we could achieve Y."
- **Reflection:** "If we had involved the team earlier, we might have avoided this."

## Practice

Write one conditional sentence each for: a risk, a proposal, and a reflection on a past project.
