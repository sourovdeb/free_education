# Hedging Language — Being Diplomatically Precise | Business English B2

**Level:** B2 | **Audience:** Professionals | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Hedging language

---

## Hedging lets you be less direct without being dishonest.

It signals uncertainty, softens strong claims, and shows professional or academic caution.

### Modal verbs
- "This **could** indicate..."
- "It **may** be that..."
- "The results **might** suggest..."

### Adverbs
Possibly · probably · seemingly · arguably · apparently

### Reporting verbs
- "It **appears** that..." · "It **seems** as though..." · "Research **suggests**..."

### Distancing phrases
- "To some extent..." · "In many cases..." · "It could be argued that..."

## When NOT to hedge

When you need to be directive: "You **must** submit this by Friday." Not: "You might want to consider submitting this by Friday."

## Practice

Rewrite with appropriate hedging:
1. "The new system will increase productivity by 30%."
2. "The problem is caused by poor communication."
