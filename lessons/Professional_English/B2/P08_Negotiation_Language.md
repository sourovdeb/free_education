# Negotiation Language | Business English B2

**Level:** B2 | **Audience:** Professionals | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Negotiation

---

## Negotiation requires precision, flexibility, and face-saving language.

### Opening positions
- "Our initial position is..."
- "We were hoping for..."
- "Ideally, we would be looking at..."

### Making concessions
- "We could be flexible on [x] if you could move on [y]."
- "If you are able to meet us halfway on the timeline, we could consider..."
- "We might be able to accommodate that, provided..."

### Signalling limits
- "That is our bottom line on this."
- "I am afraid we cannot move further on price."

### Buying time
- "Could we come back to you on that?"
- "I would need to check with my team first."

## Key principle

Always link concessions: "If you can do X, we can do Y." Never give without getting.

## Practice

Negotiate a 10% discount and extended payment terms on a supplier contract. Use at least 3 phrases from above.
