# Telling the Time — O'clock and Half Past | English for Kids A1

**Level:** A1 | **Audience:** Kids (primary) | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Telling the time

---

## What time is it?

- 3:00 → **"It's three o'clock."**
- 3:30 → **"It's half past three."**

## Ask and answer

"What time is it?" → "It's ___."

## Practice

1. 7:00 → *It's seven o'clock.*
2. 9:30 → *It's half past nine.*
3. 12:00 → *It's twelve o'clock.*

## In real life

What time do you **wake up**? Go to **school**? Eat **dinner**?
