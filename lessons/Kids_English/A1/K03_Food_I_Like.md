# Food I Like and Don't Like — English for Kids | A1

**Level:** A1 | **Audience:** Kids (primary) | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Food vocabulary + simple present

---

## Say what you like and don't like!

- "I **like** pizza."
- "I **don't like** onions."
- "**Do you like** ice cream?" → "Yes, I do!" / "No, I don't."

## Food words

pizza · pasta · rice · bread · soup · cake · apples · carrots · fish · eggs

## Your turn

Write 3 sentences:
- "I like ___." · "I don't like ___." · "I love ___!"

## Ask a friend

"Do you like _____?" — they answer yes or no.
