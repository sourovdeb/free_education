# My Family — English Vocabulary Lesson for Kids | A1

**Level:** A1 | **Audience:** Kids (primary) | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Family vocabulary

---

## Learn family words in English!

- **mother / mum**
- **father / dad**
- **sister**
- **brother**
- **grandmother / grandma**
- **grandfather / grandpa**

## Say it

Point to a family member and say: *"This is my **mum**. Her name is Ana."*

## Practice

Draw your family. Write the word under each person.

Can you say: *"I have a _____ and a _____."*?

## Quick quiz

- Who is your mum's mum? → **grandmother / grandma**
- Who is your dad's brother? → **uncle**
