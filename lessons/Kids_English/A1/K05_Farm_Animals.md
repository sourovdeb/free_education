# Animals on the Farm — English for Kids | A1

**Level:** A1 | **Audience:** Kids (primary) | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Farm animals vocabulary

---

## Animals live on a farm!

**Farm animals:** cow · pig · horse · sheep · chicken · duck · goat · dog · cat

## Animal sounds

- A cow says **"moo!"**
- A pig says **"oink!"**
- A sheep says **"baa!"**
- A duck says **"quack!"**

## Make sentences

"A **cow** has **four legs**." · "A **chicken** has **two wings**."

## Try it

Draw and label four farm animals. Write one sentence about each one.

## Quiz

- Which animal gives us milk? → **cow**
- Which gives us eggs? → **chicken**
- Which gives us wool? → **sheep**
