# Colours in My World — English for Kids | A1

**Level:** A1 | **Audience:** Kids (primary) | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Colours vocabulary

---

## What colour is it?

**Colours:** red · blue · green · yellow · orange · purple · pink · white · black · brown

## Make sentences

"The apple is **red**." · "The sky is **blue**." · "The grass is **green**."

## Try it!

Look around you. Find 5 things and say: *"The [chair] is [brown]."*

## Quick quiz

- What colour is a banana? → **yellow**
- What colour is a fire engine? → **red**
- What colour is grass? → **green**
