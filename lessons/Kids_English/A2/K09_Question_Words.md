# Question Words — Who, What, Where, When, Why | English for Kids A2

**Level:** A2 | **Audience:** Kids (primary/lower secondary) | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Question words

---

## Question words start your questions!

| Word | Asks about |
|---|---|
| **Who** | people |
| **What** | things / actions |
| **Where** | places |
| **When** | time |
| **Why** | reasons |

## Examples

- "**Who** is your teacher?"
- "**Where** do you live?"
- "**Why** are you late?"

## Practice — match the question

1. ___ is your favourite food? → *What* → "Pizza!"
2. ___ do you live? → *Where* → "In London."
3. ___ is your birthday? → *When* → "In July."
