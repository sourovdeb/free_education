# Present Continuous — What's Happening Right Now? | English for Kids A2

**Level:** A2 | **Audience:** Kids (primary/lower secondary) | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Present continuous tense

---

## Use present continuous for what is happening NOW!

**Form:** am / is / are + verb**-ing**

- "I **am reading** a book." (right now!)
- "She **is writing** her homework."
- "We **are playing** football."

## Not now, but habitual?

Use simple present: "I **play** football on Saturdays."

## Practice

Look around you. Write 3 sentences: *"My [friend] is ___ing."*

## Quick check

1. He ___ (eat) lunch right now. → *He **is eating** lunch.*
2. They ___ (sleep) at the moment. → *They **are sleeping**.*
