# Simple Present — What I Do Every Day | English for Kids A2

**Level:** A2 | **Audience:** Kids (primary/lower secondary) | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Simple present tense

---

## The simple present tells us about habits and routines.

**Form:**
- I / You / We / They → **play**, **eat**, **go**
- He / She / It → **plays**, **eats**, **goes** (add -s!)

## Examples

- "I **play** football every Saturday."
- "She **reads** books every night."
- "He **goes** to school by bus."

## Watch out!

"She go" ✗ → "She **goes**" ✓

## Practice

1. He ___ (watch) TV every day. → *watches*
2. We ___ (study) English on Mondays. → *study*
