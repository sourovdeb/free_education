# My School Day — Daily Routines | English for Kids A2

**Level:** A2 | **Audience:** Kids (primary/lower secondary) | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Daily routines + simple present

---

## Talk about your day!

**Routine verbs:**
wake up · get dressed · eat breakfast · go to school · study · have lunch · play · do homework · go to bed

## Make sentences

- "I **wake up** at 7 o'clock."
- "I **eat breakfast** at 7:30."
- "I **go to school** at 8 o'clock."

## Your turn

Write **5 sentences** about YOUR school day. Use **"at"** + time.

## Tell a partner

"What do you do at 4 o'clock?"
