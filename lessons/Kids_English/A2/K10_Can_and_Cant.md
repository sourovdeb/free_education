# Can and Can't — Talking About Abilities | English for Kids A2

**Level:** A2 | **Audience:** Kids (primary/lower secondary) | **Time:** 5 minutes | **Topic:** Modal verb 'can'

---

## Use "can" and "can't" to talk about abilities!

**Form:** *can* + verb (no -s, no -ing!)

- "I **can** swim." ✓
- "She **can't** drive." (she is 8 years old!)
- "**Can** you play the guitar?" → "Yes, I can!" / "No, I can't."

## Your abilities

Write **3 things you CAN do** and **1 thing you CAN'T do** (yet!).

## Talk to a partner

"Can you ___?" — and answer honestly.

## Watch out!

"She cans swim" ✗ → "She **can** swim" ✓
