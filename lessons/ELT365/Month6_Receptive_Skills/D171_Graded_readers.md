# ELT365 · D171 — Graded readers — what they are and why they work

**Idea —** Graded readers are books written or simplified to specific CEFR or headword levels, enabling extended reading pleasure at an accessible level.

**Example —** A B1 learner reads a graded adaptation of Sherlock Holmes — familiar story, accessible language, real narrative pleasure. The level is right; motivation stays high.

**Try —** Find one free graded reader at your students' level online. Oxford Learners' Bookworm samples and British Council LearnEnglish both have free titles.
