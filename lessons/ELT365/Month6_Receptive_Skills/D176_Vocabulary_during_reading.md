# ELT365 · D176 — Handling vocabulary during reading — to pre-teach or not

**Idea —** Pre-teach only vocabulary that will block comprehension entirely. Leave the rest: inferring from context is a reading skill, not a failure.

**Example —** 'Deforestation' in a climate text may need pre-teaching. 'Rapidly' does not — context and word shape carry enough meaning for most learners.

**Try —** Go through your next reading text. Highlight only words whose absence prevents any understanding. Pre-teach those. Leave the rest for inference.
