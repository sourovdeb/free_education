# ELT365 · D172 — What makes a good reading text

**Idea —** A good classroom reading text has a genuine communicative purpose, real information value for the learner, and a level that is reachable.

**Example —** A real flyer about a local event beats a fictional company brochure written for coursebook practice: it has purpose, it is credible, it is useful.

**Try —** Rate your next reading text: does it have purpose, information value, and level match? If one criterion fails, decide whether the task compensates.
