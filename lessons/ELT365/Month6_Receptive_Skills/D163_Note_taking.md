# ELT365 · D163 — Note-taking strategies while listening

**Idea —** Effective note-taking captures key ideas and numbers using symbols and abbreviations — not whole sentences. Transcribing causes learners to miss the next idea.

**Example —** Symbol set: '>' for 'led to', '≠' for 'different from', '?' for 'unclear'. A learner who writes full sentences misses half the audio.

**Try —** Play a two-minute recording. Ask students to note only key words and symbols. Then reconstruct the main ideas aloud. Compare with the audio.
