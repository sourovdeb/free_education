# ELT365 · D161 — Authentic vs graded listening texts

**Idea —** Authentic texts are unmodified real recordings. Graded texts are scripted or adapted for a level. Both have legitimate uses in the ELT classroom.

**Example —** A genuine radio interview challenges advanced learners with authentic pace and overlap. A scripted A2 dialogue controls rate and vocabulary for beginner success.

**Try —** For your next lesson, decide: authentic or graded? Write one sentence justifying your choice. Then plan how you will support whichever you pick.
