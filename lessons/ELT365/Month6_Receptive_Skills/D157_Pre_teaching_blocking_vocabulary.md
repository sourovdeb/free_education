# ELT365 · D157 — Pre-teaching blocking vocabulary

**Idea —** Blocking vocabulary means words whose absence will stop comprehension entirely. Pre-teach only those — not every new word in the text.

**Example —** In a sports commentary, 'offside' may block a non-sports listener. 'Enthusiastically' probably will not — context carries the meaning.

**Try —** Go through your next text. Mark words that would genuinely prevent understanding. Pre-teach those. Leave the rest for learners to guess from context.
