# ELT365 · D152 — What receptive skills are

**Idea —** Receptive skills — reading and listening — involve decoding meaning from input. The learner receives language; they do not need to produce it.

**Example —** A student watches a short video clip and answers one question about the topic. They processed real language without speaking a single word.

**Try —** Give any learner a short text. Ask them to tell you one thing they understood after one read. No writing. No speaking. Pure decoding.
