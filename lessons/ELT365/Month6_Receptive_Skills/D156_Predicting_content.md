# ELT365 · D156 — Predicting content before listening or reading

**Idea —** Prediction activates background knowledge and creates a purpose for engaging with the text. It primes the brain to recognise incoming information faster.

**Example —** Before a listening about nutrition, students write three things they predict they will hear. During the task, they check their predictions against the audio.

**Try —** Before your next text, stop. Ask students: 'What do you think this will say?' Collect three predictions. Let the text confirm or surprise them.
