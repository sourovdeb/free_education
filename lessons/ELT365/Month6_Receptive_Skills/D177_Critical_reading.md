# ELT365 · D177 — Critical reading — beyond the literal

**Idea —** Critical reading means evaluating the writer's purpose, assumptions, and evidence — not just retrieving information. It is usually taught at B2 and above.

**Example —** A student asks not just 'What does the writer say?' but 'Why is the writer saying this? Who benefits? What is not being said?' That is critical reading.

**Try —** Take any opinion piece. Ask: what is the writer not saying? What assumption sits under the headline? Discuss with a colleague before using with students.
