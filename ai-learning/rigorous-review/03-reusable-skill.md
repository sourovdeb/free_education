# Keep one reusable skill and adapt its setup

## The suggestion and its reason

You could keep one canonical Rigorous Review method and place host-specific setup advice beside it. When a method is copied into many agent descriptions, a correction in one place can be missed elsewhere. A canonical text gives you a clear place to inspect changes.

## The package

`SKILL.md` describes the skill and contains its canonical instructions. `prompts/CORE_INSTRUCTIONS.txt` holds the copy-ready core. Supporting references cover examples, checks, evaluation and model/host adaptation. The supplied integrity test checks that the core in the two locations matches.

You could load supporting material when needed instead of pasting the whole archive into every task. This depends on the actual host: a reference file's existence is not proof of automatic loading.

## The proposed method

The method defines the question and source mode, checks access and context, builds the evidence record, assesses and integrates claims, challenges and corrects when justified, calibrates the conclusion, returns the six headings and inspects the result before stopping.

The separation helps distinguish gathering evidence from deciding what it warrants. It preserves corrections, rejected options and unresolved assumptions rather than letting later summaries turn them into facts.

## What to preserve

You could retain the three source modes, six-heading output, meaningful qualifications and distinction between proposed and completed actions. For a smaller model, the package suggests narrower stages, explicit choices and a checked example. That is a design proposal to evaluate, not evidence of larger-model equivalence. Broader synthesis still needs source-support checks.

## Why runtime instructions remain direct

Reader-facing prose explains choices as suggestions. Exact runtime instructions still need clear actions and failure conditions. Making “do not invent a citation” optional would change the method rather than merely soften its tone.

You can choose whether to use the method. Once selected, its honesty and source-preservation requirements define the intended behaviour.

## Limits and a first step

The skill is intended for evidence reviews, contested claims and defensible decisions, not unrelated creative writing, translation or simple editing. It grants no browsing, execution, private-account access or persistent memory.

You could try the canonical core in one actual host, retain the answer and record setup-specific adjustments separately.
