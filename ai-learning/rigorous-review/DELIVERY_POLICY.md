# Delivery convention for this educational resource series

User-requested convention: turn related educational creations into individual WordPress resource pages, with a dedicated hub where useful. Reuse a matching existing page after reading it. Keep new website content in draft unless publication is explicitly authorised. Retain a labelled video section without inventing a video or embedding a placeholder URL.

Present reader-facing recommendations as suggestions, with their reason and material limitations. Preserve exact technical contracts, code, source meaning and test-status evidence.

For a requested delivery, save readable resources to `sourovdeb/free_education`, provide verified download links for PDF/ZIP assets, and email the authenticated owner when sending is authorised. Resolve the owner's email through the connected Gmail account rather than committing a private address here.

The repository is public; exclude unrelated personal records, credentials and third-party material outside the authorised resource scope. A WordPress draft is not a public page, but downloadable media URLs can be publicly accessible.

Before repeating a delivery, inspect existing pages, repository files and the delivery record. Preserve a video, transcript or user edits that have since been added. Compare actual source versions rather than duplicating an unchanged resource.

This is a saved procedure, not an installed scheduler or a guarantee of cross-conversation memory. No recurring process has been enabled by this document. A future session needs access to it and the relevant creation and connectors.
