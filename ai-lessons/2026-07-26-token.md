## 📖 WHAT IS A TOKEN?

**Simple Definition:**
A token is a small chunk of text that AI breaks language into for processing. Think of it like currency—AI "spends" tokens to read and write.

**The Basic Rule:**
- Roughly 4 characters = 1 token
- Roughly 100 tokens = 75 words
- "Hello world" = 2 tokens
- "Artificial intelligence is fascinating" = 6 tokens

**Why do tokens matter?**
Because AI doesn't see text like you do. It breaks everything into pieces, processes them, and counts how many pieces it used. More pieces = more processing = higher cost.

**The Hidden Truth (What AI Companies Won't Emphasize):**
Tokens are how AI companies charge you. Every word you send and every word you get back costs tokens. Understanding tokens means understanding how much your AI usage actually costs and what your limits are.

## ⚙️ HOW DOES IT WORK?

### The Mechanism (Behind the Scenes)

**How AI counts tokens:**

```
YOUR INPUT:
"What is machine learning?"

         ↓

AI TOKENIZES:
Token 1: "What"
Token 2: "is"
Token 3: "machine"
Token 4: "learning"
Token 5: "?"

         ↓

AI PROCESSES:
(Uses 5 tokens from your input)
(Thinks...)
(Generates response)

         ↓

AI'S RESPONSE:
"Machine learning is..."

         ↓

AI TOKENIZES RESPONSE:
Token 1: "Machine"
Token 2: "learning"
Token 3: "is"
... [continues]

TOTAL RESPONSE: 87 tokens

         ↓

FINAL COUNT:
Input: 5 tokens
Output: 87 tokens
TOTAL COST: 92 tokens
```

### Why Tokens Instead of Words?

AI breaks text into tokens because:
1. **Punctuation counts separately** — "Hello, world!" = 4 tokens, not 2 words
2. **Numbers and special characters cost tokens** — "2026-07-26" = 4 tokens
3. **Some words split** — "butterfly" = 1 token, but "antidisestablishmentarianism" = 8 tokens
4. **Languages vary** — English is efficient (100 words ≈ 75 tokens), but Chinese uses more tokens per word

# Tokens: How AI Counts Words (And Why It Costs)

## The Quick Answer

A **token** is a small chunk of text that AI breaks language into for processing.

Roughly 4 characters = 1 token.

Roughly 100 tokens = 75 words.

**Why this matters:** AI charges you by the token. More tokens sent and received = higher cost. When you hit your token limit, the conversation ends.

## Picture It This Way

Imagine you're texting a friend, but you pay per character sent and per character received.

- Short text (10 characters) = costs 3¢
- Long text (500 characters) = costs $1.50
- Super long message (5,000 characters) = costs $15

That's how tokens work with AI. Every piece of text you send and get back costs money.

**More text = higher bill.**

## What is a Token?

### The Simple Version

A token is how AI breaks text into bite-sized pieces for processing.

```
Text:      "Hello, world!"
Tokens:    ["Hello", ",", "world", "!"]
Count:     4 tokens
```

### Real Examples

| Text | Token Count |
|------|-------------|
| "Hi" | 1 |
| "Hello world" | 2 |
| "How are you today?" | 5 |
| "The quick brown fox" | 4 |
| "Artificial intelligence is fascinating" | 6 |
| "2026-07-26" | 4 |

**Key insight:** Punctuation, numbers, and special characters count as separate tokens.

### Why Not Just Count Words?

AI uses tokens instead of words because:

1. **Punctuation matters** — "Hello!" isn't the same as "Hello"
2. **Numbers take space** — "2026-07-26" is one date but multiple tokens
3. **Languages differ** — English needs ~75 tokens per 100 words, but Chinese needs more
4. **Processing reality** — AI actually processes tokens, not words
