# Open Teacher Training v3 — Curriculum

Source edition of the curriculum in the master ZIP. This is a curriculum candidate, not an accredited or delivered 200-hour course. No Cambridge affiliation or CELTA award is implied.

Twelve modules are preserved. Four new literacy packs and the retained M02-U01 instruction pack are complete samples. Forty-three other units remain specifications. Educator review is pending for every sample.

## M01 — Understand learners

| Unit | Learning design | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| M01-U01 Diagnose reading needs | Compare decoding, vocabulary and comprehension. Separate unfamiliar script from limited English. | A learner profile supported by word, sentence and text tasks. |
| M01-U02 Diagnose writing needs | Compare planning, sentence formation, spelling and communication. Ask about purposes and schooling. | A writing sample annotated by difficulty. |
| M01-U03 Identify access barriers | Test keyboard, screen, print and audio routes. Ask; do not infer disability or attention from age. | Two task versions and an access plan preserving the outcome. |
| M01-U04 Set learning goals | Translate observed needs into actions. Distinguish CEFR-informed tasks from validated levels. | Two goals and a baseline task. |

Sources: C1, D2, R9 in SOURCES.md.

## Activity budget

Study and demonstration 40 hours; observation 20; planning/materials 60; real teaching 12; feedback/revision 40; assessment/portfolio 28. Total: 200 hours. Count activities once. These are design estimates, not equivalent Cambridge contact hours or guaranteed mentoring.

## Preserve URLs during migration

M02-U01 remains M02-U01. The old objective-setting outline M01-U01 maps to M01-U04. The old feedback outline M09-U01 maps to M09-U02. The earlier checking-meaning URL remains M03-U01. Do not silently reuse identifiers for different lessons. The master package includes exact URL mappings and draft XML.

## Media and attention

The website carries the complete learning route. Print provides an offline option. Video demonstrates one decision, with captions and a transcript. LinkedIn introduces a problem and links to practice. Short entry tasks should build toward complete texts and sustained work. Proposed duration bands are pilot choices, not universal attention limits.

## Before release

Every finished unit needs real input, instructions, answer guidance, revision, access support and an unseen transfer task. No private candidate record or centre handout is reproduced here. Educator and learner trials remain pending.
