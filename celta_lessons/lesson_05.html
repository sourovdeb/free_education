Food I Love — Vocabulary for Eating and Cooking

A1–A2 ⏱ 5 minutes Vocabulary
📖 From My Life
The first Australian food that confused me: Vegemite. Dark brown paste, sold as a breakfast spread. I tried it like peanut butter — thick layer on toast. Never again. But it taught me something: food is culture. Learning food words in English means learning how people live.

🎯 Language Focus

Basic foodbread, rice, meat, fish, egg, fruit, vegetable, soup
Cooking verbsboil, fry, bake, grill, steam, roast, mix
Taste adjectivessweet, sour, salty, spicy, bitter, fresh, rich
Useful phraseI like / I don't like / I love / I can't stand + food noun

✏️ Quick Practice — Try It!

1. Which verb: pasta in hot water? boil / bake / fry
Boil ✓ — "Boil the pasta for 10 minutes.".
2. Opposite of sweet?
Sour or bitter ✓.
3. Fill: "I ___ spicy food!" (strong positive)
I love spicy food! ✓.
4. Name your favourite food. Say: I love ___ because it's ___.
Your answer — e.g. "I love mango because it's sweet and fresh.".

👶 For Young Learners

🍎 Go to your kitchen! Point at 5 foods and say their name in English. Can't find the word? That's your homework for the day!

💡 Your 5-Minute Takeaway

Use love / like / don't like / hate + noun to express food preferences instantly. Simple, clear, natural. No need for complex grammar.
