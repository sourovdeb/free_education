Present Perfect vs Past Simple — The Rule That Changes Everything

B1 Intermediate ⏱ 5 minutes Grammar
📖 From My Life
A guest at The Ivy in Sydney once asked me, "Have you ever been to Paris?" I said, "Yes, I went to Paris." She gently corrected me: "We say 'I've been to Paris' — the when doesn't matter here." That one correction from a stranger taught me more than a month of study.

🎯 Language Focus

Past SimpleFinished time matters: "I went to Paris in 2018."
Present PerfectTime is unfinished/irrelevant: "I have been to Paris." (when? doesn't matter)
Signal words (PP)ever, never, already, yet, just, recently, since, for
Signal words (PS)yesterday, last week, in 2019, ago, when I was…

✏️ Quick Practice — Try It!

1. Choose: "She ___ (see/has seen) that film last night."
saw ✓ — "last night" = finished time → Past Simple.
2. Choose: "___ you ever ___ (eat) sushi?" (ever = present experience)
Have you ever eaten sushi? ✓ → Present Perfect.
3. Correct: "I have gone to the market yesterday."
I went to the market yesterday ✓ — "yesterday" = finished time.
4. Write: Have you ever visited an island? Answer with a full sentence.
Your answer — e.g. "Yes, I have visited La Réunion." / "No, I haven't visited an island.".

💡 Your 5-Minute Takeaway

The golden rule: see a finished time (yesterday, in 2019, last year)? → Past Simple. No time, or a life experience? → Present Perfect. Ask yourself: "Does the when matter here?"
