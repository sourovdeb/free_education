Reported Speech — What They Actually Said

B1–B2 ⏱ 5 minutes Grammar
📖 From My Life
In the restaurant world, messages get passed through a chain of people. "The head chef says the fish is off." "The manager says we need to cover table 8." "A guest said she was waiting for twenty minutes." Every one of those is reported speech. It's one of the most-used grammar structures in professional English.

🎯 Language Focus

RulePresent → Past when reporting: "I am tired" → She said she was tired
Say vs Tellsay (+ words): "He said he was late." / tell (+ person): "He told me he was late."
Time shiftsnow → then | today → that day | tomorrow → the next day | here → there
Yes/No question"Are you ready?" → She asked if I was ready.

✏️ Quick Practice — Try It!

1. Report: She said, "I am happy."
She said she was happy ✓.
2. Report: He told me, "We will start soon."
He told me they would start soon ✓.
3. say or tell? "She ___ me she was leaving."
told ✓ — tell + person.
4. Report this question: "Do you speak French?"
She asked if I spoke French ✓.

💡 Your 5-Minute Takeaway

When reporting in past → shift all verbs one tense back. am→was, is→was, will→would, can→could. This backshift is the engine of reported speech.
