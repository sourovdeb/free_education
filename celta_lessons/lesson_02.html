Colours in English — A World of Colour

A1 Beginner ⏱ 5 minutes Vocabulary
📖 From My Life
The market in Saint-Pierre, La Réunion, on a Saturday morning. Red lychees, yellow mangoes, green bananas, purple passion fruit, orange papaya. When I describe it in French it takes two sentences. In English, one word per colour. Colours are one of the fastest wins in any language.

🎯 Language Focus

Neutralwhite, black, beige, cream
Key ruleIn English: colour BEFORE noun → a red car, not a car red

👶 For Young Learners

🎨 Draw a rainbow and label each colour in English: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet. Sing the rainbow song — it really works!

💡 Your 5-Minute Takeaway

Colour + noun — never the other way in English. "A blue door" not "a door blue." Once you know this rule, you know it forever.
