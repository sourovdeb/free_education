## Design Principles Applied

- **No Claude/Anthropic trademarks** — all content is independent
- **Cambridge CEFR levels** — A1 through C1, clearly labelled
- **CELTA methodology** — PPP, TBL, TTT, PWP, Guided Discovery frameworks used appropriately
- **Personal experience woven in** — anecdotes from 18 years hospitality (Sydney: Star Casino, Merivale, The Ivy, Establishment, Uccello, Popolo, Morrison), sommelier/barista training, CELTA practice, La Réunion life, multilingual experience (Bengali/English/French/Hindi), fatherhood
- **Interactive HTML** — checkboxes, colour-coded level headers, collapsible answer sections
- **Colourful design** — each level has a distinct colour palette
- **5-minute format** — explanation + 3 practice tasks + production task + self-check
- **Cambridge disclaimer** on every lesson footer
- **No duplicate content** — all 39 titles are original; existing 33 ELT Masterclass posts untouched
