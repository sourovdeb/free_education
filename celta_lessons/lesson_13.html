Phrasal Verbs at Work — The Hospitality Edition

B1 Intermediate ⏱ 5 minutes Vocabulary
📖 From My Life
Fourteen years in hospitality across Sydney — The Star Casino, Merivale venues, bar shifts, restaurant floors. In that time I realised that hospitality English is 50% phrasal verbs. "Set up the tables." "Hand in your timesheet." "Fill in the booking form." "Turn the music down." They're everywhere.

🎯 Language Focus

set upto prepare / arrange → "Set up the tables before guests arrive."
turn down / turn upreduce / increase → "Turn down the music." "Turn up the heat."
fill incomplete a form → "Please fill in your details."
check in / check outarrive at / depart from a hotel → standard hospitality terms

💡 Your 5-Minute Takeaway

Phrasal verbs are not random — group them by theme (work, movement, communication). Learn 3 from one topic area, use them in real sentences. That's the system.
