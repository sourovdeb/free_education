Numbers 1–20 — Count Like a Pro

A1 Beginner ⏱ 5 minutes Vocabulary + Pronunciation
📖 From My Life
At the Star Casino in Sydney, I counted covers (tables of guests) every shift. "Table 7, party of 4." "Table 13, party of 8." Numbers were not optional — one mistake and a group of twelve would be standing with no seats. Counting fast and clearly saved my job more than once.

Tricky onesthirteen (13) vs thirty (30) — stress: thirTEEN / THIRty
Pronunciation tipTeens (13–19): stress the -teen ending. Tens (30, 40…): stress the first syllable

✏️ Quick Practice — Try It!

1. Write in words: 15 + 3 = ?
Fifteen plus three equals eighteen ✓.
2. Which is bigger: thirteen or thirty?
Thirty (30) ✓ — though they sound similar!.
3. True or false: "Twelve" sounds like "two" + "-lve".
False — it's a single word: /twɛlv/.
4. Spell out your age (or a favourite number).
Your answer! e.g. "twenty-eight".

👶 For Young Learners

🎲 Play counting games! Count your toys, your steps, your snacks. The more you count in English, the faster it becomes automatic.

💡 Your 5-Minute Takeaway

The teen vs ten distinction (thirTEEN / THIRty) trips up learners at every level. Practise the stress — it prevents real-world confusion.
