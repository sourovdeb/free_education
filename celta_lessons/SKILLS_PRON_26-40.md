## Lesson 26 · Reading: Skim First, Scan Second · B1

**Personal Hook:** When studying for French C1, I had to read long articles in 40 minutes. No time to read every word. Skim for gist, then scan for detail.

| Strategy | What you do | When |
|----------|-------------|------|
| **Skim** | Read fast — titles, first lines, key words | Get the gist quickly |
| **Scan** | Search for a specific word, date, or number | Find one specific detail |

**How to skim:** Read title → first sentence of each paragraph → bold words/numbers. Do NOT read every word.

## Lesson 27 · Listening: Predict Before You Hear · B1/B2

**Personal Hook:** My daughter is 18 months old. Before she can speak, she listens with her whole body — watching faces, anticipating sounds, filling in gaps. Good listeners predict.

**How to Practise:**
1. Read the title/question first — What topic is this?
2. Think of likely vocabulary — What words might come up?
3. Consider context — Who is speaking? Where? Why?
4. As you listen, confirm or revise your predictions.

## Lesson 28 · Speaking: Tell a Story in 60 Seconds · B1

**Personal Hook:** I have told my story on three continents. This is how I structure it.

| Part | Purpose | Seconds |
|------|---------|--------|
| Context | Set the scene | ~10s |
| Event | What happened | ~25s |
| Result | The outcome | ~10s |
| Lesson | What you took away | ~15s |

**Model:** 'I was managing a team in Sydney. [Context] One evening a guest complained before we'd served the starter. [Event] We resolved it with every person on the floor. [Result] A calm voice de-escalates faster than any apology. [Lesson]'

## Lesson 29 · Formal Email Writing · B1/B2

**Personal Hook:** I have sent thousands of emails — job applications, complaints, appeals, formal requests. Each from a different country, in a different situation.

**Structure:** Subject line → Dear Mr/Ms [Surname] → Reason for writing → Body (one idea/paragraph) → I look forward to hearing from you → Yours sincerely (known name) / Yours faithfully (unknown)

| Instead of… | Write… |
|------------|--------|
| I want | I would like to / I am writing to |
| Sorry I haven't written | I apologise for the delay |
| Thanks | Thank you for your prompt reply |

## Lesson 30 · Inference in Reading · B2

**Personal Hook:** When I read Dostoevsky as a teenager — secretly, since books were not encouraged at home — I had to read between the lines. Inference is working out what the text doesn't say directly.

**Look for:** Tone · Word choice (why *stumbled* not *walked*? Why *claimed* not *said*?) · What is missing · Context

**Examples:**
- "The manager *smiled thinly* and said nothing." → She is displeased but not expressing it.
- "He *claims* to have studied at Cambridge." → The writer doubts this.
