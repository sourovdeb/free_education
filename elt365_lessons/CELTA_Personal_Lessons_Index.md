## Series Overview

These 30 learner-facing English lessons draw on the author's personal experience:
- Immigration: Chittagong (Bangladesh) → Sydney (2005–2017) → Paris/Plessis-Trévise → Saint-Pierre, Réunion
- Hospitality career: Star Casino, Merivale Group (The Ivy, Uccello, Establishment, Popolo, Morrison)
- Language journey: Bengali/English native · French C1 (DCL) · Hindi operational · Italian basics
- Family: married (Vanessa, French); father to a daughter (18 months at time of writing)

Each lesson includes: personal anecdote, grammar/vocabulary explanation, examples in context, 3 interactive quiz items (HTML `<details>` elements), and a 5-second takeaway rule.
