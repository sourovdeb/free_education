## Overview

30 five-minute interactive HTML lessons covering A1–C1, for young learners, teens, adults, and professionals.
All lessons include: colourful HTML card layout · personal story from Sourov's experience · interactive multiple-choice quiz · Cambridge level label · 2-minute Try It task.

**Personal experience threads used:**
- 18 years luxury hospitality in Sydney (Star Casino, Merivale: The Ivy, Uccello, Establishment, Popolo, Morrison)
- Immigration: Bangladesh → Sydney (2005) → France/Paris (2017) → La Réunion
- Four languages: English (native), Bengali (native), French (C1, DCL), Hindi (operational)
- Daughter born in La Réunion (~18 months at time of writing)
- Cambridge CELTA course completed — 120 supervised teaching hours, 4 written assignments validated to the required standard
- Bipolar I, ADHD, C-PTSD diagnoses (2024–25) — emotional vocabulary lesson handled with care

## Lesson Format

Each lesson is an interactive HTML post containing:

1. **Gradient header** — title, CEFR level, audience, emoji
2. 💡 **Today's Idea** — core concept (yellow card)
3. 📖 **From My Experience** — personal story (purple card)
4. 📚 **Examples / Language Focus** — blue card with tables/lists
5. ✏️ **Try It! (2 minutes)** — green card with task
6. 🎯 **Quick Quiz** — interactive multiple-choice with JavaScript feedback
7. **Disclaimer** — independence statement
