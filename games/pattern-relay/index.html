      Purpose, access, and privacy

      How to play

      1. Study the shape sequence.
      2. Keep or hide the pattern.
      3. Select shapes in matching order.
      4. Select Check pattern.
      5. Undo or reveal whenever needed.

      Use Tab, then Enter. Space also activates buttons. Dragging is never required.

      Purpose

      Matching practises checking a sequence. Hiding adds a recall requirement. Benefits outside gameplay remain untested. This is not a treatment.

      Access

      Shapes have text labels. No sound or animation plays. Memory practice is your choice. Some players may dislike repetition. Player and screen-reader testing remain outstanding.

      Privacy

      The game stores no data. The game makes no requests. A web host may log visits. Reloading clears the round.

      Feedback

      What helped you play? What created a barrier? Please avoid sharing health information.

      No timers, scores, or accounts.
