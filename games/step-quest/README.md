## Play

Download index.html and open it in your browser. Choose writing, vocabulary, or reading. Select three or five steps. Keep the plan visible, or hide it for optional recall. Select Begin, then select actions in the example order. Reveal, undo, pause, or return to setup whenever needed. Other orders can work outside this example game.

Use Tab and Enter, mouse, or touch. Dragging is unnecessary. No timers, scores, accounts, tracking, sound, animation, or saved player data. Reloading clears progress. A web host may keep its own logs.

## Purpose and limits

The game practises following an example sequence and reading action labels. Improved memory, learning outcomes, and everyday transfer are not established. It is not treatment or assessment. Reading and fixed example orders may not suit every player. No participant or screen-reader testing has occurred.

Optional practice connection: write one step for a chosen task. Keep that note visible. This is a suggestion, not a proven transfer effect.

See ../EVIDENCE.md and ../TESTING.md. The browser harness rerun passed 213 assertions. The corresponding WordPress page remains a draft with space for a video and transcript.
