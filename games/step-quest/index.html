      Purpose, access, and privacy

      Purpose

      This game practises ordering steps. Showing the plan removes memorisation. Recall mode adds that demand. Neither mode measures your ability.

      This prototype has no trials. Benefits beyond play remain untested. It does not provide treatment. It does not replace care.

      Access

      Buttons work without dragging. No sound or animation plays. Controls support touch and keyboards. Labels do not depend on colour.

      The game currently requires reading. Audio instructions are not included. Screen-reader testing remains outstanding. Feedback from players remains outstanding.

      Privacy

      The game stores no data. It makes no network requests. Hosting services may keep logs. Reloading clears your progress.

      After playing

      You may try one step. You may choose another activity. You may stop.

      Reuse

      Code uses the MIT license. Research and testing notes accompany it. This is not a diagnosis.
