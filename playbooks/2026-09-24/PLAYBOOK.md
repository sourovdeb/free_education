Use one tracker:

| Contact | Current stage | Next action | Due | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Existing conversation | Check latest reply | Complete missing step | Choose date | Receipt or reply |

Store names and correspondence privately.

**Workshop offer to test**

Audience: hospitality staff handling complaints.

Outcome: acknowledge, clarify, propose, confirm.

Format: one forty-five-minute session.

Included: practice, feedback, one handout.

Ask about their actual incidents. Ask who approves training. Ask about participants and budget. Confirm terms before accepting work. Do not invent market rates.

Six contacts is a checkpoint. Allow one follow-up per contact. Then inspect targeting and wording. Silence does not prove incompetence.

This threshold is a heuristic. Adjust it to actual capacity.
