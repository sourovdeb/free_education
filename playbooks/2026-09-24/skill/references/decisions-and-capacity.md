# Decisions and capacity

## Choose through gates

| Gate | Evidence needed |
|---|---|
| Need | Someone describes the problem |
| Reach | A legitimate contact route |
| Fit | Demonstrated ability or test |
| Capacity | Work fits existing obligations |
| Economics | Costs and payment terms |
| Maintenance | Ongoing workload is acceptable |
| Integrity | Claims and rights are supportable |

Do not multiply unknown scores. Numerical rankings can hide assumptions. Prefer explicit trade-offs and conditions.

## Livelihood branches

If essentials exceed dependable income, prioritise income closure. Keep an existing job when possible. Verify alternatives before recommending resignation. Avoid speculative product-building as rescue.

If basics are covered, allow experimentation. Set a time and money ceiling. Define a buyer or user response. Choose a date for reconsideration.

If money facts are missing, branch. Never fill missing costs with averages. Separate cash, invoices, promises, projections. Tax and benefits require jurisdiction checks.

## Capacity branches

Capacity means hours after obligations. Exclude sleep, care, and relationships. Include research, travel, review, and maintenance.

A five-hour example may allocate:

- Income: 150 minutes.
- Experiment: 60 minutes.
- Administration: 30 minutes.
- Review: 30 minutes.
- Reserve: 30 minutes.

These are heuristics, not prescriptions. Replace them with observed capacity.

If capacity halves, reduce scope. Do not halve every project mechanically. Preserve one income action and deadlines. Pause expansion before care or rest.

If capacity increases, deepen existing work. Do not expand commitments automatically. Retain stopping times and reserve.

## Experiments

Require a person, problem, and test. Reuse a working artifact first. Observe an attempt before expanding features. Define what counts as improvement. Record support given during the test.

One successful test proves little generality. It can justify the next revision. No responses may indicate targeting problems. Test contact accuracy before abandoning competence.

## Review

Ask what reached a person. Ask what generated a response. Ask what consumed maintenance. Ask what crowded out relationships. Stop one thing when overloaded.

Keep three next actions maximum. Keep evidence outside self-worth judgments. Record why a direction changed.
