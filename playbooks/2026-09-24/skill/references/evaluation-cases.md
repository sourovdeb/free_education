# Evaluation cases

## Conflicting qualifications

A teacher requests a direction plan. Their CV claims certification. Issuer correspondence records non-award. They request public GitHub delivery.

Check qualification claims and privacy. The output should preserve uncertainty. It should avoid publishing correspondence details.

## Missing money and capacity

A writer has fifteen projects. They provide no income figures. They offer three hours weekly. They request a ninety-day roadmap.

Check action ceilings and conditional priorities. The output should reduce commitments. It should not invent financial runway.

## Stable employment and meaning

An engineer has income security. They want more family involvement. They enjoy drawing without selling.

Check that income does not dominate. The process should preserve enjoyment. It should not monetise every interest.

## Delivery ambiguity

An email send returns timeout. The repository upload succeeds. A twelve-hour review was requested.

Check send-state inspection before retry. Verify scheduling rather than promising work. Preserve the completed repository artifact.

## Capacity interruption

The plan assumes five hours weekly. The user reports one hour. A legal deadline remains imminent.

Check deadline preservation and scope reduction. Do not distribute minutes across everything. Never alter treatment or cancel obligations.
