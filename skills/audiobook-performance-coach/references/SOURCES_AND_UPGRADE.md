# Sources and evidence limits

Version: 1.2.0. Review date: 24 September 2026.

## Source scope

Web pages were inspected as text. No recording was listened to.
No speech measurements were taken. Pronunciation examples remain provisional teaching guides.
Sources support principles, not guaranteed results. Performance tables are original design proposals.

| Source | Evidence used | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| [ACX: Director's Notes](https://www.acx.com/mp/blog/directors-notes-for-your-acx-production) | preparation of character, accent, names, motives, continuity | practitioner guidance; not an efficacy trial |
| [ACX: The Mate Games](https://www.acx.com/mp/blog/the-mate-games-a-romantasy-universe-comes-to-life-in-full-sound) | creators describe selective Foley with narrative purpose | one production; no universal prescription |
| [IDEA archive](https://www.dialectsarchive.com/) | recordings paired with biographies/transcripts/commentary | archive access isn't listening evidence |
| [IDEA England 1](https://www.dialectsarchive.com/england-1) | speaker history; individual variation | not all English speakers |
| [IDEA Scotland 1](https://www.dialectsarchive.com/scotland-1) | Dundee background; contact-driven variation | not a verified accent recipe |
| [IDEA Australia 1](https://www.dialectsarchive.com/australia-1) | biography and individual variation | audio features unassessed |
| [IDEA France 1](https://www.dialectsarchive.com/france-1) | multiple countries influenced speaker | phonetic commentary absent |
| [IDEA India 1](https://www.dialectsarchive.com/india-1) | commentary notes aspiration patterns for this speaker | not a Bengali-English baseline |
| [British Council: Connected Speech](https://www.teachingenglish.org.uk/professional-development/teachers/knowing-subject/connected-speech-part-1) | phrase-level pronunciation; weak forms in context | does not require maximal reduction |
| [NIDCD: Voice Care](https://www.nidcd.nih.gov/health/taking-care-your-voice) | avoid vocal overuse/extremes; rest when tired/hoarse | no individual medical assessment |

The accent guide credits IDEA. No archive audio or transcripts are redistributed.
The guide links to source pages. It uses speaker-specific evidence boundaries.

Cambridge pronunciation-page retrieval was blocked. The car/bath examples are not source-verified.
No blocked page supports a claim. No unavailable RSC material is cited.

## Design choices

These belong to this pack:

- Five-layer coaching model
- Objective, obstacle, and tactic cards
- UK-P/GA-R respelling labels
- Cue density and batch heuristics
- Situation/action tables
- Effect timing proposals
- Mixed-feeling maps
- Voice reset and continuity workflow
- JSON coverage checks

They require rehearsal to judge usefulness. They aren't Cambridge or ACX endorsements.
There is no measured energy saving. There is no model-size performance guarantee.

## Upgrade basis

The supplied 1.1.0 ZIP was inspected. Its source and examples were retained.
The previous core had one UK key. Accents lacked a continuity protocol.
Effects had recipes and feasibility rules. Scene-purpose selection needed more guidance.
Emotion cues lacked an objective/subtext layer. The upgrade adds those layers.

Version 1.2.0 preserves sentence blocks. It preserves both pronunciation and reading views.
It retains STEP and SCENE. It retains source-preservation and bounded repair.
It adds accent cards and drills. It adds sound modes and omissions.
It adds structured-score validation. Old test specifications remain identifiable.

Prior pack bibliography is historical context. Its earlier inspection claims aren't repeated here.
No private account material enters examples. Public examples are invented rehearsal fiction.
