# Reader edition: The Puppy at the Door

Version 1.2.0. Fiction written for this package.
Bracketed sounds are added presentation cues.
Technical instructions and sound-out guides are hidden.

[Rain at the glass: tik...tik-tik.]

Rain tapped against the window.

Mina held the little blanket close.

Something scratched at the door.

[Skr...skr. Then silence.]

“Please be there,” she whispered.

She opened the door.

A wet puppy stood on the step.

“I thought I’d lost you,” Mina said.

The puppy wagged his tail.

“You came back!”

Mina laughed and wrapped him in the blanket.

Outside, the rain kept tapping.

[Tik...tik-tik. Rain outside.]

Inside, everything was warm.
