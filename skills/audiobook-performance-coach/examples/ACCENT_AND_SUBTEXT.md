# One line, four takes

Version: 1.2.0. Fiction for rehearsal.
Source: “You brought the car back.”
These are alternatives, not consecutive dialogue.

## SETTINGS AND KEY

Policy: annotated. Mode: NARRATION. Effects: none.
UK-P: `kah`; postvocalic r absent.
GA-R: `kar`; pronounce the r.
Other symbols follow the accent reference.
All respellings are provisional. No recording was assessed.
Bold marks prominence, not loudness.

| Take | Accent | Situation | Objective | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | UK-P | feared loss; sees return | acknowledge the return | none |
| B | GA-R | same situation as A | same objective as A | none |
| C | UK-P | unexpected person returns it | discover their involvement | none |
| D | UK-P | person promised something else | identify the mismatch | none |

A/B changes accent only. C/D changes intention, not accent.
Read one take at a time.

## COACHING NOTES

TEXT: someone brought a car back.
CHOICE: all four proposed circumstances.
UNKNOWN: the author's actual intended context.

A needs no gasp or sob. B preserves A's timing and purpose.
C directs attention toward the person. D can communicate disappointment without shouting.
Neither C nor D requires villainy. Neither requires an accent change.

Say A, then B. Keep the intention constant.
Then say A, then C. Keep UK-P constant.
Compare what changed in meaning.
