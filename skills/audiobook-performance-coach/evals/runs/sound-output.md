## SETTINGS AND KEY

A single unaided mouth cannot provide an independent, continuous rain sound beneath spoken narration. This usable solo version alternates brief mouth-made rain with speech. Continuous overlap would require another sound source.

- **Mode:** STORYTELLING; annotated source; both scripts; three-sentence STEP.
- **Rain:** your requested added atmosphere, labelled **CHOICE**. The passage itself supplies no weather.
- **Narrator:** address one listener; draw attention to the sound, hold attention through the wait, then identify what appears. Use an even conversational pace and a compact pitch range. No character dialogue is present.
- **Accent:** keep your own. The pronunciation script uses provisional **UK-P**, a non-rhotic teaching reference, rather than an assessment of your accent. Respelling is approximate. Your supplied **NEE-luh** governs Nila.
- **Notation:** bold marks phrase focus; capitals in respelling mark syllable stress. Bracketed directions are not narrated. Read **one** script per take.

Key used here: `uh` = opening of *about*; `ee` = *see*; `i` = *sit*; `e` = *bed*; `ay` = *day*; `eye` = *my*; `aw` = *thought*; `ow` = *now*; `dh` = *this*. Other consonants have their usual sounds; hyphens divide syllables. In `bi-HEYEND`, `eye` supplies the vowel in *my*.

## COACHING NOTES

The rain is a stylised substitute: use gentle breath and light tongue releases, with no throat scraping. Let each effect finish before forming the next word. The recurring texture can suggest ongoing rain, but it will be audibly absent during narration. Its absence is a performance constraint, not an assertion that the weather changes.

**FX01:** purpose = establish the requested rain motif; basis = CHOICE, your request; method = mouth-made “tss…tss-tss”; anchor = before “A” in P01-S01; duration = proposed 1.2 seconds; level = quieter than your speaking voice; perspective = general atmosphere, no invented story location; exit = stop before speech and breathe comfortably; fallback = omit.

**FX02:** purpose = recall the rain motif during the wait; basis = CHOICE, your request; method = the same mouth sound; anchor = after “waited.” in P01-S02; duration = proposed 1.2 seconds; level = as FX01; perspective = unchanged; exit = stop before “A” in P01-S03 and breathe comfortably; fallback = omit.

Both effects belong to you and require no equipment. The sound durations occupy the gaps; they are not additional silent pauses. Repeating their IDs in the pronunciation script represents the same two events.

Keep the scrape unidentified. Avoid an animal sound before the kitten appears, and do not imply that the text confirms the kitten caused the scrape. Fear, relief, and affection are optional interpretations, so this score leaves them open.

Before a take, reset with “A scrape came from behind the wall.” in your ordinary speaking voice. For an effects-off version, omit FX01 and FX02 and use comfortable sentence breaks. If the mouth effect feels uncomfortable or obscures the words, omit it.
