# One Lesson, Three Languages

Free access needs translation.

A link is insufficient.

Learners need usable language.

They need local context.

They also need downloads.

ELT365 can test this.

## The pilot

One lesson comes first.

Three versions will follow.

The languages are English.

French will support Réunion.

Bengali will widen access.

Each version stays editable.

Each version works offline.

## The seven-day test

Day one confirms rights.

Day two builds terminology.

Days three and four translate.

Day five reviews language.

Day six tests access.

Day seven invites feedback.

The pilot stays small.

That keeps costs controlled.
