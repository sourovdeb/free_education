Handle a guest complaint

Practise listening through reading, clarifying, apologising, and offering action.

Goal

By the end, you can respond to one hotel complaint using four moves.

  1. Listen.
  2. Clarify.
  3. Acknowledge.
  4. Offer action.

Model dialogue

Guest: Excuse me. The air conditioner is making a loud noise.

Staff: I’m sorry about that. Is the noise constant?

Guest: Yes. I couldn’t sleep.

Staff: I understand. I can send maintenance now, or arrange another room.

Guest: Another room, please.

Staff: Certainly. I’ll check availability now.

Phrase bank

Clarify
When did it start?
Clarify
Is it happening now?
Acknowledge
I understand the problem.
Apologise
I’m sorry about that.
Act
I can send someone now.
Act
I can offer another room.

Check meaning

A guest says, “The shower has no hot water.” Which response should come first?

Which sentence offers action?

Build your response

Situation: A restaurant guest waited forty minutes. Their meal arrived cold.

Role-play

Learner A: Choose one complaint.

  • Noisy room.
  • Cold meal.
  • Missing booking.

Learner B: Use all four moves.

Observer: Tick each move heard.

□ Clarify □ Acknowledge □ Apologise □ Act

Exit check

Record one response aloud. Keep it under thirty seconds. Ask a partner whether every move was clear.
