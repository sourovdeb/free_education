# Why I Should Not Apply For Funding Yet

A real funding opportunity exists.

That does not mean I should apply today.

The Wikimedia Foundation offers Rapid Funds between USD 500 and USD 5,000. Individuals may apply. Education projects can qualify. Translation and project-management roles may receive support.

The next deadline is 1 November 2026.

Those facts sound encouraging. The eligibility rules matter more.

Applicants need current contribution history on their target Wikimedia project. They also need organising or training experience. People without active Wikimedia engagement must involve relevant community members or affiliates.

I have teaching experience.

My Wikimedia history remains unproven.

That gap changes the plan.

## What I will build first

I will begin with Wikibooks.

Wikibooks accepts instructional textbooks, guides, manuals, and self-learning materials. That gives Business English a possible home beyond my website.

Source: [Wikibooks policy](https://en.wikibooks.org/wiki/Wikibooks:What_is_Wikibooks)

My first draft covers complaint handling in hospitality. Learners practise four moves:

1. Clarify.
2. Acknowledge.
3. Apologise.
4. Act.

I will place the chapter inside a user sandbox. Then I will request community feedback.

## What funding could support

A future project could expand one Wikibooks guide, create French adaptations, train adult contributors, and publish reusable workshop materials.

It cannot exist mainly to improve my website.

Wikimedia excludes projects aimed at improving third-party websites. It also excludes paid editing.

Source: [Wikimedia Rapid Fund](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Grants:Programs/Wikimedia_Community_Fund/Rapid_Fund)

## My seven-day test

- Create one Wikimedia account.
- Read the target policies.
- Improve two existing pages.
- Add one sandbox draft.
- Request community feedback.
- Contact the regional officer.
- Check eligibility honestly.

France belongs to Wikimedia's Northern and Western Europe region. Réunion's treatment should still be confirmed directly.

Source: [NWE funding region](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Grants:Regions/Northern_and_Western_Europe)

## The decision rule

I will apply only when:

- Contributions are public.
- Feedback exists.
- Training evidence exists.
- Community members participate.
- The project supports Wikimedia.
- The budget funds future work.

Otherwise, I will wait.

Free education needs funding.

It also needs trust.
