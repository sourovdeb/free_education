# AI and learning access

## Evidence

- AI can widen practice access.
- Designed tutors can help.
- Outcomes depend on design.
- Unguarded chat can harm.
- Models can produce errors.
- Training data carries bias.
- Access gaps still matter.
- Privacy needs protection.

## Inference

- Hints can preserve effort.
- First attempts reveal thinking.
- Independent explanations show learning.
- Human review protects agency.

## Unproven claims

- AI democratises all learning.
- Chatbots replace qualified teachers.
- Fluent answers are accurate.
- Cheap access ensures equality.

## Scope limits

- Both trials used narrow settings.
- Neither tested English learning.
- Transfer needs further study.
- Product behaviour can change.
- Policies can also change.
