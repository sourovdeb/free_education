# Episode 02 sources

## Evidence

- Rome invaded during 43 CE.
- Rule ended early 400s.
- Vindolanda 234 uses Latin.
- It dates 97–105 CE.
- Verbeia's altar uses Latin.
- Verbeia names local worship.
- Old English dates later.
- Settlement began during 400s.

## Inference

- Latin gained public visibility.
- Institutions produced more writing.
- Brittonic speech likely continued.
- Bilingual lives were likely.
- Records favor powerful writers.

## Unproven

- Exact speaker shares remain unknown.
- Latin replaced Brittonic entirely.
- English began one day.
- One language ruled everywhere.
