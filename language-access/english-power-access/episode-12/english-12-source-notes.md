# Recognition without prestige

## Evidence

- Recognition routes differ.
- French service rules differ.
- Réunion hiring lists requirements.
- Registration means no approval.
- ORCID identifies researchers.
- DOI identifies records.
- Review evaluates claims.

## Inference

- Portfolios reveal real practice.
- Revisions reveal professional judgment.
- Multiple reviewers reduce bias.
- Visible evidence builds trust.

## Unproven claims

- Prestige proves teaching ability.
- Identifiers prove research quality.
- Portfolios replace legal requirements.
- Visibility guarantees fair recognition.

## Scope limits

- Rules can change.
- Roles have separate requirements.
- Portfolios need external review.
- Review can remain biased.
- Titles need honest descriptions.
