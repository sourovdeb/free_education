# Episode 10 sources

## Evidence

- CELTA has defined criteria.
- Teaching practice gets assessed.
- Written assignments also count.
- External moderation samples work.
- IELTS scores language performance.
- CEFR supplies broad descriptors.
- Observation reliability varies.
- More occasions can help.

## Inference

- Brands reduce review costs.
- Verification supports institutional trust.
- Combined evidence improves judgment.
- Costs can exclude talent.

## Unproven

- Prestige guarantees competence.
- One score proves teaching.
- One lesson predicts careers.
- Any system removes bias.

- IELTS does not assess teaching.
- CEFR levels remain broad.
- Observation needs repeated evidence.
