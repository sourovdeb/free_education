Where English began

Episode 1 of 12. English, power, access.

English has no birthday

Germanic settlers reached Britain. Their dialects shaped Old English. Settlement began during the 400s. The language changed across generations.

A family, then dialects

Old English belonged to West Germanic. Old Frisian was related. Old Saxon was related. English later developed several dialects. West Saxon dominates surviving texts.

Some words stayed

Compare hand with hand. Compare word with word. Both appear in Old English. Their sounds may have changed.

Manuscripts came later

Speech preceded surviving manuscripts. Cædmon's Hymn survives in copies. Researchers found another Rome copy. It dates from 800–830.

The limits matter

Texts reveal what survived. First conversations left no records. No single inventor appears. That conclusion is an inference.
