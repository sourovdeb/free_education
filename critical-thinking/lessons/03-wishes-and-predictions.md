# CT03 — Keep Wishes Separate from Predictions

[Course index](../README.md) · Day 3 · SORT

## Today’s rule

A wish can define a goal; it cannot establish a forecast. Keep what you value, what you expect, and what you will try separate.

## Today’s task

Choose one ambition. Finish: “I want ___.” “I predict ___ because ___.” “My next small test is ___.” List one condition that already exists and one that is missing.

## Why this matters

A plan can become misleading when hoped-for money, time or demand is treated as already available. A missing condition does not by itself prove an ambition impossible either.

## Hypothetical example

Goal: build an educational course. Prediction: it will attract a large audience quickly. Plan: produce one useful lesson, ask for feedback, and record the work required. The pilot can teach you about the plan without guaranteeing future demand.

## Self-check

Have you explained what supports the prediction? Keep hoped-for income separate from confirmed resources. Avoid both “I want it, therefore it will happen” and “It is not established yet, therefore it cannot happen.”
