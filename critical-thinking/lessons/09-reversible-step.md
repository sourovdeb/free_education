# CT09 — Take a Reversible Step and Inspect the Result

[Course index](../README.md) · Day 9 · DECIDE

## Today’s rule

Choose an appropriate next step without pretending uncertainty has disappeared. A precaution can make sense even when failure is not known to be likely.

## Today’s task

Write: “The evidence supports ___.” “My next action is ___ because ___.” Name the main downside, reversibility and the result that would count as success. Consider the cost of delaying as well as acting.

## Hypothetical example

Test a file exporter on a copy. Before running it, specify the expected destination and records. Afterward, inspect the file. A success message with empty output is not success at the task. Do not label unexecuted code as tested.

## Why this matters

A plan needs a feasible action and an observable result. When giving advice, check the person’s resources and constraints rather than saying “just do it.”

## Self-check

Could your check detect the original failure? Are other people’s interests or permissions involved? A reasonable next step may be a small test, one missing fact, qualified review, or no action—not necessarily the largest intervention.
