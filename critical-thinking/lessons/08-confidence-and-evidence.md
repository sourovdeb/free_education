# CT08 — Match Confidence to Evidence

[Course index](../README.md) · Day 8 · VERIFY / DECIDE

## Today’s rule

Confidence needs a reason. Fluent language, authority and speed are not substitutes for evidence.

## Today’s task

Finish: “My confidence is ___ because ___.” Name the main uncertainty precisely: not supplied, not inspected, not verified, or genuinely disputed. Do not invent a percentage to make an impression sound measured.

## Hypothetical example

An AI answer arrives quickly and includes impressive-looking references. Only the production of the answer has been established. Its decisive references and claims still need checking. A short answer might be quick and correct; a polished answer might be wrong. Neither speed nor length settles the issue.

## Why this matters

How likely an event is and how confident you are in that estimate are different questions. “Uncertain” does not automatically mean “50–50.” A bounded answer can be more defensible than an unsupported guarantee.

## Self-check

Does your confidence statement identify actual support and its limit? Do not say you searched, tested or verified something unless that work happened. An instruction to check an answer is not proof that a check occurred.
