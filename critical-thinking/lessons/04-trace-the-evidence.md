# CT04 — Trace a Claim to Its Evidence

[Course index](../README.md) · Day 4 · VERIFY

## Today’s rule

Trace the important claim, not the number of repetitions. Several reports can depend on one original assertion.

## Today’s task

Select a claim from an article, conversation or AI answer. Record its exact wording, the source you can inspect, and the passage that supports it. Does the passage establish the whole claim or only part? When a source is unavailable, write “not inspected” rather than filling the gap.

## Why this matters

Repeated wording does not create independent evidence. Reputation does not replace checking whether a source’s evidence fits this claim.

## Synthetic practice fixture

Article A quotes a company announcement. Article B summarizes Article A. Article C links to the same announcement. How many independent observations of the product’s effectiveness are shown? None are established by this fixture; three articles repeat one announcement.

## Self-check

Can you show the supporting passage and identify its origin? Do not turn “I did not find evidence in the material checked” into “there is no evidence anywhere.”
