# CT07 — Use the Same Standard in Disagreement

[Course index](../README.md) · Day 7 · CHALLENGE

## Today’s rule

Check the claim rather than your liking for the speaker. Fair scrutiny means consistent standards, not automatic agreement or equal weight for unequal evidence.

## Today’s task

Choose a low-stakes disagreement. Restate the other person’s actual argument in terms they could recognize. Identify one supported point and one unresolved question. Ask: “Would I accept this reasoning from someone I disagree with?”

## Hypothetical example

Disliking a speaker does not establish that their statistic is false. Admiring a speaker does not establish that it is true. Trace the number and inspect its scope separately from evaluating conduct.

## Why this matters

You can concede a valid criticism without conceding every disputed issue. Improve the judgment rather than protect an identity or win an exchange.

## Self-check

Have you preserved the actual argument rather than replacing it with an easier target? Relevant differences can justify different conclusions; name them. For politics, compare documented facts without endorsing choices. For religion, distinguish historical, empirical, ethical and metaphysical claims rather than forcing them into one test.
