# CT06 — Test a Plausible Alternative

[Course index](../README.md) · Day 6 · CHALLENGE

## Today’s rule

Test the strongest relevant alternative, not a convenient weak one. An event happening after another does not by itself establish causation.

## Today’s task

Choose one explanation you accept. Write a plausible alternative and one observation or safe test that could distinguish them. Do not invent an alternative merely to sound balanced.

## Hypothetical example

“Sales rose after we changed the logo, so the logo caused the increase.” The increase may be real. Advertising, pricing, seasonality or distribution changes could also matter. Check what else changed and whether a useful comparison is available. This example does not establish which explanation is correct.

## Why this matters

Evidence that fits several explanations may not distinguish them. Ask what would favor one explanation over another.

## Self-check

Have you identified a genuine competing explanation and a discriminating check? An incomplete argument does not prove its conclusion false. Strong evidence and unsupported speculation do not deserve equal weight.
