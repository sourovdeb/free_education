# CT10 — Record What Would Change Your Mind

[Course index](../README.md) · Day 10 · REVIEW

## Today’s rule

Write down what would change your answer before defending it indefinitely. Update for evidence or a better argument, not merely for pressure to agree.

## Today’s task

Choose a decision from the course. Complete the six-part record in a sentence or two per heading. Name a specific event, piece of evidence or argument that would make you revisit it.

### Bottom line

What is the most defensible answer?

### Basis

Which evidence and considerations support it?

### Main uncertainty

What objection, alternative or missing information matters?

### Confidence

How strong is the support, and why?

### Next action

What is the most appropriate feasible step?

### Reconsider when

What evidence, event or argument would change the answer?

## Hypothetical example

“I will test one lesson before building the whole course. I know the topic and have time for a pilot; audience demand and production effort remain uncertain. I will reconsider the scope after actual learner feedback and a record of the time required.”

## Why this matters and self-check

Keep what you originally knew separate from later learning. A favorable result does not by itself validate a weak process; an unfavorable result does not by itself invalidate a reasonable one. Is the review trigger specific? Writing a date does not schedule a reminder. Repeat the practices using new situations, not a demand to reach the same answer.
