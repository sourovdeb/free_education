# CT05 — Check the Numbers and the Context

[Course index](../README.md) · Day 5 · VERIFY

## Today’s rule

A number needs units, a comparison and a context. A lower package price or impressive percentage does not settle a decision by itself.

## Today’s task

Check one purchase, bill or numerical claim. Write the quantity, unit, period and assumption that matter. Recalculate the comparison. For a purchase, compare the amount you realistically expect to use, not only the amount in the package.

## Hypothetical worked example

A 1 kg pack costs EUR 3.20. A 2 kg pack costs EUR 5.60, or EUR 2.80 per purchased kilogram. If 0.5 kg of the larger pack will be wasted, its usable cost is EUR 5.60 / 1.5 kg = approximately EUR 3.73 per used kilogram. Assuming the smaller pack is fully used, it has the lower usable cost.

## Why this matters

Correct arithmetic can answer the wrong question. Changing the waste assumption changes this comparison. Storage, suitability, preparation effort and cash available also matter.

## Self-check

Can someone reproduce the calculation? Extension: EUR 8 per month for twelve months is EUR 96; six such subscriptions total EUR 576, assuming unchanged prices and continuous billing. These are arithmetic examples, not a personal financial recommendation.
