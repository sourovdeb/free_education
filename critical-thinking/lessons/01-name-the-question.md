# CT01 — Name the Question Before Answering

[Course index](../README.md) · Day 1 · FRAME

## Today’s rule

Name the question before trying to answer it. State the decision, the available options, and what would count as a satisfactory outcome.

## Today’s task

Choose one small decision. Write: “I need to decide whether ___ or ___, given ___, by ___.” Add the one fact that would most help you choose. Do not expand a small choice into a research project.

## Why this matters

An accurate answer can be irrelevant to the decision. A clear question shows which evidence matters and when you have enough information to stop.

## Hypothetical example

Instead of “Which notebook is best?”, ask: “Which of these two notebooks fits my bag, costs no more than my stated limit, and has paper I like?” Either may be adequate. Personal preference does not need proof of universal superiority.

## Self-check

Can another person tell what you are deciding and what would settle it? “The best” without criteria is unfinished. You are done when you have one answerable question, not when you have investigated every possibility.
