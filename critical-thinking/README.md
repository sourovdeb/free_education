# Critical Thinking — 10 Daily Practices

**Education edition 1.0 | 5 October 2026 | Website status: draft only**

One practice a day. One real question. One record of what you learned.

This course adapts the user-supplied **Critical Thinking and Decision Audit** material. Its method is **FRAME → SORT → VERIFY → CHALLENGE → DECIDE → REVIEW**. It is a proposed educational procedure, not a validated psychological assessment, a promise of improvement within ten days, or a substitute for subject knowledge.

## Start here

Work through one practice each day, then repeat the sequence with new examples. Five to ten minutes is a suggested starting allocation, not a deadline for serious investigation. A notebook is enough; AI is optional. On a difficult day, write the question and one unknown. Do not create a catch-up backlog.

## Publication and privacy

This repository is public, including its branches and draft pull requests. Only shareable educational text belongs here. WordPress content stays draft until the owner explicitly approves publication. Existing live pages and menus were not rewritten.

The earlier version 2.0 ZIP was reported to contain 75 files, but its backing archive was not retrievable during this delivery. This edition is **not** that archive, a 75-file migration, or a new 74-entry audit. Private transcripts, signed attachment links, account screenshots, and credentials are excluded. The original illustrated PDF and available source files are retained outside the public edition.

No Mini/Mistral behavioral runs, independent evaluations, automatic future synchronization, or scheduled daily emails are established by these files. See the final delivery record for actions actually completed.
