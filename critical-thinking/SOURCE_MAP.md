# Source Map and Scope

## Basis

S1: User-supplied `critical-thinking/SKILL.md`, titled Critical Thinking and Decision Audit. Read in full for the education adaptation. It labels its procedure proposed rather than validated.

S2: User-supplied `Pasted markdown.md`, containing the critical-thinking explanation and the earlier illustrated-guide discussion. The requested six-part format is preserved verbatim in the worksheet.

S3: User-supplied `skill_library_upgrade_master_prompt.md` and `chatgpt_mini_honesty_quality_skill_questions.md`. Used to describe the review workflow and the separation of design review, actual target-model testing and independent evaluation. No new full audit is claimed.

S4: Available original `Mistral_Critical_Thinking_Build_Guide.pdf`. Preserved outside the public edition because it contains account screenshots. The public companion is grounded in S2 and is not a reproduced or newly verified PDF.
