## The disturbing truth

**Moral certainty can precede reasons.**

Kin cues shape judgment.

They need no calculation.

People may feel certainty.

Reasons can come later.

Yet this remains debated.

## What research found

Lieberman tested co-residence.

Third-party judgments were measured.

Longer co-residence predicted opposition.

Genetic relatedness was controlled.

Believed relatedness mattered less.

The study supported Westermarck.

Later work partly replicated.

Fessler found similar patterns.

Kibbutz studies added evidence.

Antfolk tested disgust ratings.

Co-residence increased reported disgust.

Biological relatedness also mattered.

Family relationship mattered too.

Recent evidence continues.

Babchishin studied 1,623 adults.

Longer cohabitation predicted less interest.

Attraction patterns moderated effects.

So cues are conditional.

## What this does NOT prove

**Taboos are biologically fixed.**

Culture can redirect behavior.

Institutions can change norms.

People differ widely.

Aversion can fail.

Abuse still occurs.

## Why this discomforts us

We prefer moral reasons.

We distrust bodily intuitions.

We expect family protection.

Evidence complicates both beliefs.

Moral certainty has mechanisms.

Family closeness has limits.
