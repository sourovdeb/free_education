## The buried mechanism

An emergency appears.

Shared responsibility feels smaller.

Action can slow.

This is diffusion.

Ambiguity adds another layer.

Everyone can misread together.

Fear can also matter.

Relationships can matter.

Danger can matter.

The mechanism stays conditional.

## What experiments found

Classic studies found inhibition.

Fischer pooled later evidence.

They analyzed 105 effects.

They covered 7,700 participants.

Helping declined overall.

The effect was modest.

Danger weakened that effect.

Physical support changed choices.

Known bystanders changed choices.

So groups are complex.

They can inhibit action.

They can enable action.

## Real violence changed expectations

CCTV research added realism.

Philpot studied 219 conflicts.

Three cities were included.

At least one intervened.

That happened 90.9 percent.

Several people often intervened.

More bystanders sometimes helped.

That challenges simple pessimism.

Another study found nuance.

Individual helping still dropped.

Social relationships mattered more.

Danger also increased intervention.

Then came newer evidence.

It appeared September 15.

Commercial robberies were studied.

Only 35 percent helped.

Weapons were more common.

Fewer bystanders were present.

Context changed the outcome.

No universal rate exists.

## What this does NOT prove

## Counterargument

So pessimism also misleads.
