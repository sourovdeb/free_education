# The Default

Truth comes first.
Doubt often comes later.
Truth-Default Theory explains this.
People usually accept messages.
Suspicion needs a trigger.
That trigger may fail.
This is not stupidity.
Trust saves mental effort.
Social exchange also depends.
Constant checking has costs.
Two experiments tested this.
Participants heard plausible stories.
Some stories were lies.
Researchers tracked spontaneous thoughts.
Veracity checking appeared rarely.
It stayed under ten percent.
Another meta-analysis studied judgments.
It covered 206 documents.
It included 24,483 judges.
Mean accuracy reached 54 percent.
Truths were easier.
Lies were harder.
People classified 61 percent correctly.
That applied to truths.
Lie detection reached 47 percent.
This creates discomfort.
We value personal discernment.
We trust our instincts.
Evidence gives less confidence.
The default also helps.
Most daily messages cooperate.
A suspicious society stalls.
The mechanism is mixed.
It supports social function.
It also creates openings.
Skilled deception can exploit them.

Evidence: Default acceptance appears common.

Interpretation: Trust supports cooperation.

Uncertainty: This remains theoretical.
