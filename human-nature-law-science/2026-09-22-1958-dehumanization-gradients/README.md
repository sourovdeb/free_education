## The buried mechanism

**The disturbing truth:**

Humanness becomes a sliding scale.

**What this does NOT prove:**

Dehumanization causes all violence.

People classify other people.

Most classifications stay harmless.

Some classifications change concern.

Some change perceived mind.

Some change perceived worth.

That process has levels.

### Level one

A person seems distant.

Their feelings matter less.

Their inner life fades.

### Level two

A group seems simpler.

Individual stories disappear.

### Level three

Human traits get denied.

Warmth gets denied.

### Level four

## Psychology lens

Haslam reviewed dehumanization.

He proposed two forms.

One form is animalistic.

Another is mechanistic.

Both deny humanness.

Neither requires open hatred.

That point matters.

Dehumanization can stay ordinary.

It can stay subtle.

Kteily tested blatant forms.

Seven studies were reported.

Three countries were included.

Blatant dehumanization predicted aggression.

It predicted punitive support.

It exceeded prejudice sometimes.

A 2025 meta-analysis followed.

It pooled 184 effects.

Thirty-two papers were included.

The overall association was moderate.

Reported r was 0.34.

Blatant forms were stronger.

Subtle forms still mattered.

But association is limited.

It does not prove causation.

## Why this unsettles

Hatred is not required.

Rage is not required.

A person can disengage.

A group can become abstract.

Concern can then shrink.

The mind may comply.

No dramatic moment occurs.

That makes detection difficult.

## Language matters
