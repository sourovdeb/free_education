Which Platform Changed?

A station notice changes one detail.

Use four steps:

1. Match destination and date.
2. Find “today only”.
3. Use platform 5.
4. Separate boarding and departure.

Model:
“The train leaves from platform 5 today. Boarding closes at 14:15.”
