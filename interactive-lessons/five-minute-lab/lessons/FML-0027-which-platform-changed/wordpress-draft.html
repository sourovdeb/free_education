Which Platform Changed?

Goal: Find destination, exception, platform, and boarding-time evidence in a station notice, then state the current travel details.

Situation

The 14:20 train to Saint-Pierre usually leaves from platform 2. Today only, it leaves from platform 5. Boarding closes at 14:15.

Model

“The train leaves from platform 5 today. Boarding closes at 14:15.”

Choose

Which platform should you use today?

  • Platform 2.
  • Platform 5.
  • Platform 14.

Answer: Platform 5. The exception overrides the usual platform.

Try it

An airport shuttle usually uses bay 3. A notice says: ‘Monday only: Bay 7. Boarding closes 08:40. Departure 08:45.’

Possible answer: “The shuttle leaves from bay 7 today. Boarding closes at 08:40.”
