Find the Words That Prove It

Goal: Use question keywords to scan a short notice, select relevant evidence, and give an evidence-based answer.

Older teenagers and adults. A2-B1 estimate. 3-5 minutes.

Prerequisites: Can read common times; Can understand short public notices

The shuttle notice

AIRPORT SHUTTLE CHANGE. Friday only. The airport shuttle leaves from Gate B, not Gate A. Last departure: 18:40. Hotel guests need a blue ticket.

Where should you wait? Gate B. Evidence: The airport shuttle leaves from Gate B, not Gate A.

Question-to-evidence map

QUESTION: Where should you wait? -> SCAN: look for gate -> EVIDENCE: leaves from Gate B, not Gate A -> ANSWER: Gate B.

First identify the type of text and the practical situation.

Meaning, form, context

Form: Short notices often omit full sentences: Last departure: 18:40 means The last departure is at 18:40. Your answer may be short if the evidence is copied clearly.

Meaning: Evidence is the exact part of the notice that makes an answer reasonable. The answer Gate B is supported by the line naming Gate B as the departure point.

Reasoning: 18:50 is after 18:40, so a traveller arriving at 18:50 cannot take the last shuttle described by this notice.

Context: For practical notices, scan for the detail asked about rather than reading every word at the same speed. Copy only enough text to prove the answer without changing its meaning.

Try a library notice

LIBRARY - SATURDAY. Open: 09:00-13:00. Help desk closes at 12:30. Returns box stays open all day.

At 12:45, can you ask the help desk for assistance? Answer yes or no. Then copy the words that prove your answer.

Nothing is saved. Nothing is graded.

Show self-check
  • The answer is no.
  • The evidence names the help desk.
  • The evidence includes 12:30.
  • The answer connects 12:45 with the earlier closing time.
  • Clear valid variants are accepted.

Possible answers:

  • No. Evidence: Help desk closes at 12:30.
  • No - the notice says, 'Help desk closes at 12:30.'
  • You cannot ask the help desk at 12:45 because it closes at 12:30.

Other clear answers can work.

Reading mode

This remains usable without scripts.

Where should you wait for the shuttle?

  • Gate B Yes. The notice says the shuttle leaves from Gate B, not Gate A.
  • Gate A No. Gate A appears only as the old or rejected location. The words not Gate A rule it out.
  • The hotel lobby No. The notice mentions hotel guests but gives no lobby departure point.

Which words prove the last shuttle leaves before 19:00?

  • Last departure: 18:40. Correct. This line gives the last time, and 18:40 is twenty minutes before 19:00.
  • Friday only. This gives the day, not the departure time. It cannot prove the before-19:00 answer.
  • Hotel guests need a blue ticket. This gives a ticket rule. It does not contain time evidence.

You arrive at 18:50. Can you take this shuttle?

  • No - the last departure is 18:40. Yes. The answer and copied time agree: 18:50 is after the last departure.
  • Yes - 18:50 is before 19:00. No. The relevant boundary is 18:40, not 19:00. Use the notice's exact cutoff.
  • Yes - it is Friday. Friday is required, but the arrival is still ten minutes after the last departure.
