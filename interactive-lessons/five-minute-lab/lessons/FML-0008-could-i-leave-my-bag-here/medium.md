# Could I Leave My Bag Here?

**Goal:** Ask permission to leave an item until a stated time.

## Situation

Checkout is at 11:00.

Your train leaves at 15:30.

You want hotel storage.

```text
PERMISSION       ACTION + OBJECT       PLACE       TIME LIMIT
Could I      +    leave my bag     +    here    +  until three?
```

## Model

**Could I leave my bag here until three?**

### Grammar

After **could**, use the base verb.

Use **leave**, not **left**.

### Meaning

**Could I...?** asks permission.

Here, **leave** means putting the bag somewhere where it stays.

**Until three** means up to 3:00.

### Appropriateness

**Could I...?** is polite.

**Can I...?** is also common.

**May I...?** is more formal.

## Follow the answer

Reception says:

**Yes, you can leave it behind reception.**

A useful response is:

**Thanks. I'll collect it at three.**

## New situation

You need to leave a parcel at reception until 4:00.

Write one permission question.

Possible answers:

- Could I leave this parcel here until four?
- Can I leave this parcel here until four?
- Is it okay if I leave this parcel here until four?

## Self-check

- Permission is requested.
- Action and object are clear.
- The time limit is clear.
