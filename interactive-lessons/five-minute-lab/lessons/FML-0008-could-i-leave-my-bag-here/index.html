Could I Leave My Bag Here?

Goal: Use Could I + base verb + object + place + until + time? to ask permission in a travel situation.

Older teenagers and adults. A2-B1 estimate. 3-5 minutes.

Prerequisites: Can understand simple hotel situations.; Knows common place and time words.

The journey

Checkout is at 11:00. Your train leaves at 15:30. You want the hotel to keep your bag.

Could I leave my bag here until three?

The gap

Meaning, form, context

Form: Could + subject + base verb forms a permission question. No do/does is added.

Meaning: Could I...? asks permission. Here, leave means put an item somewhere where it stays.

Time: Until three means the bag may stay there up to three o’clock.

Context: Could I...? is a polite permission request. Can I...? is also common. May I...? is more formal.

Reading mode

This remains usable without scripts.

Which sentence asks permission?

  • Could I leave my bag here until three? Yes. Could I asks permission. Leave stays in the base form.
  • Could I left my bag here until three? After could, use the base verb: leave, not left.
  • Could you leave my bag here until three? This asks the other person to do something. The target asks permission for your action.

What does until three mean here?

  • The bag can stay there up to 3:00. Yes. Until marks the end point of the time period.
  • The bag must arrive at exactly 3:00. No. Until describes how long the bag may stay.
  • The bag leaves before checkout. No. The request is for storage after checkout.

Reception says: Yes, you can leave it behind reception. What response fits?

  • Thanks. I'll collect it at three. Yes. It acknowledges permission and confirms the collection time.
  • Thanks. I collected it at three. That sounds like a past event. The collection is still in the future.
  • Could I leave your bag here? That changes the object. You are talking about your own bag.
