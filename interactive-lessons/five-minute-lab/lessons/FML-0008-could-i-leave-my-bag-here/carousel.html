01 / THE GAP

Checkout is at 11:00. Your train leaves at 15:30. You want the hotel to keep your bag.

02 / BUILD IT

PERMISSION: Could I | ACTION + OBJECT: leave my bag | PLACE: here | TIME LIMIT: until three

03 / FORM + TIME

Could + subject + base verb forms a permission question. No do/does is added. Until three means the bag may stay there up to three o’clock.

04 / YOUR TURN

After a meeting, you need to leave a parcel at reception until 4:00. Write one permission question.
