## Reported speech: questions

British Council LearnEnglish

https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2-grammar/reported-speech-questions

Accessed: 2026-09-28

Inspected: The page explains that indirect questions retain the question word but use statement word order.

Limitation: It supports the embedded-question explanation, not a billing procedure.

## Hotel booking websites: compliance principles for businesses

UK Competition and Markets Authority

https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/hotel-booking-websites-compliance-principles-for-businesses

Accessed: 2026-09-28

Inspected: The official principles discuss clear presentation of total costs and additional charges in hotel booking contexts.

Limitation: This is UK business guidance. The lesson uses it only to support the value of written price evidence and does not teach legal rights.
