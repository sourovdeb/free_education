The Shower Isn't Working

Goal: Use item + isn't working, one evidence detail, and a polite request to report a hotel problem and confirm the next step.

You are in room 214. The shower runs, but the water stays cold.

OBSERVE: no hot water -> REPORT: The shower isn't working -> REQUEST: Could you send someone, please? -> CONFIRM: room 214, about ten minutes.

The shower isn't working. There's no hot water. Could you send someone to look at it, please?

Form

The shower isn't working uses subject + is not + verb-ing. The shower doesn't work is also possible; isn't working foregrounds the problem happening now.

Meaning

Here, work means operate correctly. The evidence no hot water explains the exact malfunction.

We'll send someone in ten minutes gives a waiting period. Confirm the room and approximate time so both people share the same next step.

Appropriateness

State the item and evidence before asking for action. Could you send someone to look at it, please? is a polite request; Can you send someone? is also normal and direct.

Practice

Which report matches the evidence?

  • The shower isn't working. There's no hot water. Yes. It names the item, reports the current malfunction, and adds useful evidence.
  • The shower doesn't like me. No. A shower cannot like or dislike someone. Report what the device does or does not do.
  • The shower was dirty yesterday. This describes a different past problem. It does not report today's cold water.

Which order builds the current problem?

  • The + shower + isn't + working. Correct. Put the item first, then is not, then the -ing form.
  • The + shower + not + working is. This order is not a standard statement. Keep isn't before working.
  • Isn't + the shower + work. This resembles a question but mixes forms. Say The shower isn't working.

Reception says, 'We'll send someone in ten minutes.' Which reply confirms the plan?

  • Thank you. Room 214, in about ten minutes? Yes. It checks both the room and the approximate response time.
  • So I should fix it myself? No. Reception has offered to send help; this changes the agreed action.
  • You sent someone yesterday. This looks backward and does not confirm today's room or waiting time.

Your turn

The heater in room 318 blows cold air. Reception says a technician can come in fifteen minutes.

Write a short report with one evidence detail. Add a polite request. Then confirm room 318 and the fifteen-minute wait.
