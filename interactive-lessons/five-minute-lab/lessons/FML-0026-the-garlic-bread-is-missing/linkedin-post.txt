The Garlic Bread Is Missing

One order item is absent.

Use four steps:

1. Check the receipt.
2. Name the missing item.
3. Choose replacement or refund.
4. Confirm the details.

Model:
“My order is missing the garlic bread.”

Then choose:
“Could you send a replacement, please?”
