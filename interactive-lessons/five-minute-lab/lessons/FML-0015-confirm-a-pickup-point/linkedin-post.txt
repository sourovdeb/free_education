Not the Main Entrance - the Taxi Rank

One wrong place can cause a missed pickup.
Correct the place, then confirm the landmark and time.

Correct the place:
“Sorry, not the main entrance - the taxi rank beside the blue clock.”

Then confirm the full pickup point:
“So you'll meet me at the taxi rank beside the blue clock at 8:15, right?”

Try it with a hotel pickup:
side entrance beside the pharmacy at 6:40.
