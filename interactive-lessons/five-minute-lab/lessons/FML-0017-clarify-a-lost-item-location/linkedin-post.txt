Was It on the Bus or at the Station?

A useful lost-item report separates place, position, and identifying features.
Use evidence to choose the vehicle, describe the item, then confirm the search details.

Clarify the place:
“I think I left it on the number 12 bus, not at the station.”

Describe the item:
“It's a small black backpack with a red zip tag and a front pocket.”

Then confirm the search:
“So that's bus 12, under a seat near the back, correct?”

Try it with an umbrella:
train 6, overhead rack, blue curved handle, and a name label.
