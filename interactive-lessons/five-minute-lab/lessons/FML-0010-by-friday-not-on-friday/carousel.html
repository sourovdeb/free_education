01 / THE MESSAGE

Your manager writes: ‘Please send the revised rota by Friday.’ You need the exact deadline before promising delivery.

02 / THE DIFFERENCE

Picture a line running toward Friday. ‘By Friday’ points to the line’s end: finish before or at that boundary. ‘On Friday’ circles Friday itself: the event happens that day. Adding ‘3 p.m.’ makes the boundary visible.

03 / CONFIRM IT

Just to confirm, do you need it before Friday starts, or anytime on Friday? By 3 p.m. Friday, please. Understood. I’ll send it no later than 3 p.m. Friday.

04 / YOUR TURN

A colleague writes: ‘Can you share the figures by Tuesday?’ Write one reply. Confirm the cutoff. Then state when you plan to send the figures.
