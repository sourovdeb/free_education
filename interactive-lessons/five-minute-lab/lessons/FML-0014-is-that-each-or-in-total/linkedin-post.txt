Is That €60 Each or in Total?

One price can have two scopes.
Is it for each person or everyone?

Ask about the scope:
“Is that €60 per person or €60 in total?”

Then confirm the whole booking:
“So that is €180 in total for three people, right?”

Try it with:
€45 per person for two people.
