01 / THE CLOCK

It is 10:08. Your train leaves at 10:20. The station is eight minutes away.

02 / THREE VERBS

IN TIME -> catch the train | TOO LATE -> miss the train | TRAVEL CHOICE -> take the train

03 / THE CONTRAST

Catch and take can both be valid with train. Catch foregrounds making the scheduled service. Take is neutral about travel choice.

04 / YOUR TURN

A bus leaves at 18:05. It is 17:55. Write one sentence using catch, take, or miss. Make the meaning clear.
