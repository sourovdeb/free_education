Catch It or Miss It?

Goal: Choose and use catch, take, or miss with scheduled transport according to meaning.

It is 10:08. Your train leaves at 10:20. The station is eight minutes away.

IN TIME -> catch the train | TOO LATE -> miss the train | TRAVEL CHOICE -> take the train

If I leave now, I can still catch the 10:20 train.

Practice

You have twelve minutes. Which sentence focuses on being in time?

  • I can still catch the 10:20 train. Yes. Catch focuses on reaching the scheduled train in time.
  • I can still miss the 10:20 train. Miss means arriving too late for the train.
  • I can still train the 10:20. Train is not the transport verb needed here.

Which sentence is neutral about the travel choice?

  • I usually take the train to work. Yes. Take can state the transport choice without focusing on being just in time.
  • I missed the train to work. This says you arrived too late for it.
  • I caught the train to work. This is valid English, but catch makes successful boarding part of the meaning.

It is 10:18. You are still at the hotel. What now fits?

  • I might miss the 10:20 train. Yes. Miss fits the risk of arriving too late.
  • I might catch the hotel. Catch needs the transport service here, not the hotel.
  • I might take late. Take needs a transport noun phrase, such as take the train.

Your turn
