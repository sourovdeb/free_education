Tuesday, Not Thursday

Goal: Use Sorry, I meant X, not Y and a short confirmation question to repair one wrong booking detail.

You booked Tuesday at 7:30. Reception repeats Thursday at 7:30. Only the day is wrong.

HEAR: Thursday at 7:30 -> SPOT: the booking says Tuesday -> CORRECT: Tuesday, not Thursday -> CONFIRM: Tuesday at 7:30, right?

Sorry, I meant Tuesday, not Thursday. So that is Tuesday at 7:30, right?

Form

I meant uses the past form of mean to correct an earlier message. X, not Y contrasts the intended detail with the mistaken one.

Meaning

Tuesday is the intended day; Thursday is the detail to replace. The confirmation question checks the final shared version.

So that is Tuesday at 7:30, right? repeats both the repaired day and the unchanged time before the conversation ends.

Appropriateness

Sorry softens the interruption. A calm voice and stress on Tuesday help signal a repair rather than blame. Sorry - Tuesday, not Thursday is also a suitable shorter repair.

Practice

Booking card: TUESDAY, 7:30. Reception says Thursday. Which detail needs repair?

  • The day Yes. The card and reception disagree about Tuesday versus Thursday; both give 7:30.
  • The time No. Both versions say 7:30. Keep the matching detail and repair only the day.
  • Both day and time No. Changing 7:30 would create a new mistake. Repair only the evidence that conflicts.

Which repair clearly replaces the wrong detail?

  • Sorry, I meant Tuesday, not Thursday. Yes. It gives the intended day first, rejects the mistaken day, and stays polite.
  • No. You are wrong. This signals disagreement but does not supply the correct day. It can also sound blaming.
  • Sorry, I meant Thursday, not Tuesday. This reverses the evidence. Put the intended detail before not and the mistaken detail after it.

Reception accepts the correction. Which reply checks the final booking?

  • So that is Tuesday at 7:30, right? Yes. It confirms the repaired day and the unchanged time together.
  • Thursday is fine. No. This accepts the mistaken day and conflicts with the booking evidence.
  • What time is Tuesday? This is unclear. A day does not have one time; repeat the full booking detail instead.

Your turn

You asked for room 214. Reception says room 240. The room number is the only mismatch.

Write one polite repair. Then write one question that confirms the final room number.
