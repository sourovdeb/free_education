5-Minute English Lab · FML-0007

Pay Now or at Checkout?

Goal: Ask when payment happens.

1. Situation

Your room is booked.

Payment timing is unclear.

BOOKING → STAY → CHECKOUT ↓ Do I pay now or at checkout?

Meaning: the question compares two procedures.

2. Choose the question

Which sentence checks payment timing?

3. Follow the answer

Reception says:

At checkout.

What does that mean?

4. Try a new situation

At a cafe, payment is unclear.

Write one two-option question.

Possible answers:

  • Do I pay here or at the table?
  • Should I pay here or at the table?

Check three things:

  • It asks a procedure.
  • It gives two options.
  • The verb form works.
