5-Minute English Lab | FML-0002

You hear a booking time.
You think it is right.
But one mistake matters.

Try this check:

Is that 7:30 a.m. on Thursday?

Or, in conversation:

So, that's 7:30 a.m. on Thursday, right?

The skill is simple:
hear -> identify -> repeat -> confirm.

New task:
Your appointment is Monday, 2:15 p.m.
How would you confirm it?
