Could You Spell the Street Name?

You hear most of a delivery address.
One street name is unclear.

Ask for that detail:
“Could you spell the street name, please?”

Then read everything back:
“So that's 14 Rue des Alizes, 97460 Saint-Paul, right?”

Try it with:
27 Avenue des Palmiers, 97420 Le Port.
