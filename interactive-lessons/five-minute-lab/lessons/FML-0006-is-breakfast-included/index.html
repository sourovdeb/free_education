Is Breakfast Included?

Goal: Use Is + service + included? to check a package, then respond accurately to included or extra-cost information.

Older teenagers and adults. A2-B1 estimate. 3-5 minutes.

Prerequisites: Understands common hotel services.; Recognises simple questions with be.

The missing detail

Your room costs €80. The booking lists Wi-Fi, but says nothing about breakfast.

Is breakfast included?

The gap

Room: €80. Wi-Fi: included. Breakfast: not listed.

The booking shows some package details, but breakfast is unknown.

Meaning, form, context

Form: For this yes-no question, put is before the subject: Is + breakfast + included?

Meaning: Included means the service is already part of the price or package.

Answer: A reply such as No, breakfast costs €12 means breakfast is available, but its price is extra.

Context: The direct question is polite and normal at reception. Could you tell me if breakfast is included? is a softer, longer alternative.

Reading mode

This remains usable without scripts.

Which sentence checks the package?

  • Is breakfast included? Yes. Is comes before breakfast, and included asks whether the service is part of the price.
  • Breakfast is included. This is a statement. It assumes the answer instead of checking it.
  • Does breakfast included? Use the be verb here: Is breakfast included? Do not add does.

Which order builds the question?

  • Is + breakfast + included? Correct. The be verb comes first, then the service, then included.
  • Breakfast + included + is? This order does not form a standard yes-no question. Move is to the front.
  • Included + breakfast + is? Start with is, not included: Is breakfast included?

Reception says, 'No, breakfast costs €12.' Which reply fits?

  • Thanks. I'll add breakfast. Yes. This shows you understood breakfast costs extra and states your decision.
  • Great. Breakfast is free. No. A €12 price means breakfast is not included.
  • Thanks. Breakfast starts at twelve. No. €12 is the price, not the breakfast time.
