# Is Breakfast Included?

FML-0006 | v1.1.0 | A2-B1 estimate | 3-5 minutes

**Goal:** Use Is + service + included? to check a package, then respond accurately to included or extra-cost information.

## Situation

Your room costs €80. The booking lists Wi-Fi, but says nothing about breakfast.

**Model:** Is breakfast included?

## Request map

€80 PACKAGE: room + Wi-Fi | UNKNOWN: breakfast? -> ASK: Is breakfast included? -> ANSWER: included or extra cost -> DECIDE.

## Form

For this yes-no question, put is before the subject: Is + breakfast + included?

## Meaning

Included means the service is already part of the price or package.

A reply such as No, breakfast costs €12 means breakfast is available, but its price is extra.

## Appropriateness

The direct question is polite and normal at reception. Could you tell me if breakfast is included? is a softer, longer alternative.

## Which sentence checks the package?

- **Is breakfast included?** Yes. Is comes before breakfast, and included asks whether the service is part of the price.
- **Breakfast is included.** This is a statement. It assumes the answer instead of checking it.
- **Does breakfast included?** Use the be verb here: Is breakfast included? Do not add does.

## Which order builds the question?

- **Is + breakfast + included?** Correct. The be verb comes first, then the service, then included.
- **Breakfast + included + is?** This order does not form a standard yes-no question. Move is to the front.
- **Included + breakfast + is?** Start with is, not included: Is breakfast included?

## Reception says, 'No, breakfast costs €12.' Which reply fits?

- **Thanks. I'll add breakfast.** Yes. This shows you understood breakfast costs extra and states your decision.
- **Great. Breakfast is free.** No. A €12 price means breakfast is not included.
- **Thanks. Breakfast starts at twelve.** No. €12 is the price, not the breakfast time.

## Your turn

Your airport hotel booking does not mention airport pickup. Reception says pickup costs €20.

Write one included-question. Then add one short sentence accepting or declining the €20 service.

- The question names the service.
- The question checks the package.
- The word order is usable.
- The decision matches extra-cost information.
- A clear alternative is accepted.

Possible answers:

- Is airport pickup included? Thanks. I'll add it.
- Is the airport pickup included? Thanks, I won't add it.
- Could you tell me if airport pickup is included? Okay, I'll arrange another ride.

Other clear answers can work.
