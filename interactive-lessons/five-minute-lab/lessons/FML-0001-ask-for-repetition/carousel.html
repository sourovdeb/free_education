5-MINUTE ENGLISH LAB · FML-0001 · PANEL 1

Sorry, Could You Say That Again?

Situation: The receptionist says: Breakfast starts at six [noise] in the Palm Room. You missed the time.

HEAR -> MISS DETAIL -> ASK -> HEAR AGAIN -> CONFIRM
A2-B1 estimate · 3-5 minutes · Owner review required

PANEL 2 · CHOOSE

You missed the time.

A. What?
B. Could you say the time again, please?
C. Breakfast is good.
Choose before reading Panel 3.

PANEL 3 · EXPLAIN

B fits this goal.

It names the missing detail.

It asks for repetition.

Pattern: Could you + base verb + ... ?

After hearing the detail:

Six forty-five, right?

Can also works. Could often sounds more polite. Tone still matters.

PANEL 4 · TRANSFER

Try another situation.

A café worker tells you: Your table is outside, number fourteen. You hear the location, but not the number.

Your task:

Say or write one sentence asking for the number again. Then add one checking sentence after you hear: fourteen.
