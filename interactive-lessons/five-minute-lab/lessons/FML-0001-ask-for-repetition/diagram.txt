REPAIR A MISSED DETAIL

[HEAR] -> [MISS ONE DETAIL] -> [ASK] -> [HEAR AGAIN] -> [CONFIRM]

Prose equivalent:
When one detail is missed, ask for that part again.
After hearing it, confirm the detail before continuing.

Alt text:
A five-step path: hear a message, miss one detail, ask for repetition, hear the detail again, confirm it.
