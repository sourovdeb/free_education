5-Minute English Lab · FML-0001

Sorry, Could You Say That Again?

A2-B1 estimate3-5 minutesUse English

Goal: Ask someone to repeat one missed detail, then confirm what was heard.

1. The situation

The receptionist says: Breakfast starts at six [noise] in the Palm Room. You missed the time.

Your job: Ask for the missing time without restarting the whole conversation.

2. Repair the gap

When one detail is missed, ask for that part again. After hearing it, confirm the detail before continuing.

Model: Could you say the time again, please?

3. Make three decisions

4. Notice the language

Meaning: Use a repetition request when words were not heard clearly. Use a checking question after hearing the detail again.

Form: Could you + base verb + ... ?

Context: Can and could both work for requests. Could often sounds more polite. Tone and context also matter.

Different problem: If you heard the words but do not understand their meaning, ask for clarification instead of repetition.

5. Try a new situation

A café worker tells you: Your table is outside, number fourteen. You hear the location, but not the number.

Task: Say or write one sentence asking for the number again. Then add one checking sentence after you hear: fourteen.
