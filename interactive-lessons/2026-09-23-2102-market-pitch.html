Make a market pitch.

Offer a product clearly.

Goal: give a 20-second pitch.

01 · Order

Build the pitch

Tap the lines in a useful order.

“It costs five euros.”
“Good morning. I have fresh mangoes today.”
“Would you like to try one?”
“They are sweet and ready to eat.”

02 · Notice

Four useful moves

greet → name product → give benefit → invite action

Examples: fresh · local · ready today · made by hand · try one · ask a question
