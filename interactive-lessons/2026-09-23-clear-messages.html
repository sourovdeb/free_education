Clear Messages.

Reschedule a meeting. Keep trust.

Goal: write one short message.

01 · Notice

Which message works?

You missed a 10:00 interview. Choose your reply.

02 · Build

Use four parts

Greeting → reason → request → thanks

Write your message below. The checker finds each part.

03 · Say it

Read with calm stress

I apologise for missing the meeting. Could we reschedule?

Use a quiet voice. Pause after “meeting.”
